The **Redbrands** were a gang comprised of entirely humans that wore red-dyed leather armor. Under the orders of the [[Black Spider]], they terrorized much of [[Phandalin]]. Their hideout was located in an old wine cellar under the ruins of [[Tresendar Manor]]. Their leader was a human wizard named [[Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek|Glasstaff]], who was promptly executed by decapitation at the hand of [[Sildar Hallwinter]] upon his capture and transport to the [[Phandalin Townmaster's Hall]]. After their leader's death, there have been no witness sightings of the **Redbrands**.
