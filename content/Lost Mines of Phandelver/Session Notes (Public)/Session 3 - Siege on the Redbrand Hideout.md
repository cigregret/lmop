### Session Short Overview
- The trio woke from their slumber in the tavern, walked downstairs with [[Doormat]] to get their breakfast from [[Gunther]]. 
	- As they are getting breakfast, [[Fry]] notices a blur of dark colors zip by both [[Archibald]] and [[Madame Wonder]]
- This blur turns out to be a thief who made off with 82 gold, running towards the [[Phandalin Townmaster's Hall]]. [[Madame Wonder]] and [[Archibald]] chase after angrily, leaving [[Gunther]] with their food for the time being. 
	- [[Fry]] decides to go down the street alone and check out the [[Sleeping Giant Tavern]], thanks to a tip [[Gunther]] gave the night before
- [[Madame Wonder|Madame]] and [[Archibald]] find themselves outside the [[Phandalin Townmaster's Hall]], panting. As they walk inside, they see this mystery thief talking to none other than [[Sildar Hallwinter]] and a seemingly druidic dwarf. 
	- [[Sildar Hallwinter]] turns to the two and laughs, asking if their pockets felt a bit light. He goes on to explain that this was a little prank/test he set up since it took "much too long" for them to wake up. He promptly paid back the stolen gold, and introduced the two to [[Shayla]] and [[MGs]], who are [[Lord's Alliance#Organization|Crows]] within the [[Lord's Alliance]].
	- [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] decides that the two should accompany the main trio while they attempt to locate the [[Redbrands]]' hideout. He also shares that the party should spent time exploring [[Phandalin]] to hopefully find out any rumors of their hideout. He then promises the adventurers a reward of 200 gold each, to which they accept. They then walk back to the [[Stonehill Inn & Tavern]] to pay for their meals and find [[Fry]]. When they aren't able to, they figure that he has taken the initiative and gone to the [[Sleeping Giant Tavern]] to investigate further, so they head that direction.
- Meanwhile, [[Fry]] has managed to stay effectively hidden despite his plate armor, and peers through a boarded-up window of the [[Sleeping Giant Tavern]]. He sees one individual inside: the same bald human from the chessboxing match. As he recognizes this [[Strange Redbrand]], it vanishes. At that very moment, [[Fry]] hears a voice coming from behind him, on the street.
	- The [[Strange Redbrand]] does not identify himself, his powers, or answer any of [[Fry]]'s questions. Instead, he simply states that he has taken an interest in [[Fry]]'s "potential", and that "working for the [[Redbrands]] is a chore nowadays". He then proceeds to tell [[Fry]] that the [[Redbrands]]' hideout is underneath [[Tresendar Manor]], just east of town. He also gives [[Fry]] one piece of advice: "Be wary of the rat."
	- As the rest of the party begins to see [[Fry]] talking to the [[Strange Redbrand]], the [[Strange Redbrand]] takes notice and his leave, vanishing without a trace. [[Fry]]'s attempts to detect magic were not fruitful.
- Sildar then does introductions again, and encourages the newly bonded party to visit the [[Tresendar Manor]] immediately. He also explains that he was sent here by the [[Lord's Alliance]]with a mission; to find and rescue [[Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek|Iarno Albrek]], a human wizard who is of the same organization. He went missing recently, and the [[Redbrands]] seem like a good lead to start with.
- The party makes their way to the [[Tresendar Manor]] and into the cellar, and quickly finds a secret door. This door leads to a crevasse, splitting this old cellar and crypt in two. However, due to the presence of an unnatural force they retreat hastily and meet up on the seemingly intended path. Avoiding traps and defeating [[Redbrands]] and reanimated skeletons on the way, the party finds an old prison cell containing two dwarves.
	- [[Fry]] and [[MGs]] both rush to unlock the door with whatever means possible, freeing their racial kin and escorting them back to the cellar entrance where [[Doormat]] is standing guard. As the party regroups, they quickly have a run-in with the same unnatural force
	- They learn this force to be a nothic; an aberration of a former wizard that learned secrets beyond its comprehension. They attempt to strike a deal with this nothic: several barrels of salted beef and pork in exchange for safe passage. After some deliberation, the nothic agrees to these terms.
- The party, however, finds yet another secret door leading into a stairwell, which they choose to explore first. After finding the other secret door at the bottom, they open it to find a short, human male, with a dark beard, who is wearing a cloak with the [[Redbrands]]' insignia. As they open the door, this wizard jumps from his desk and casts mage armor on himself; surprised, yet nonetheless preparing for battle.
