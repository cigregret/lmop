### Short Summary
- Archibald is summoned, which seems to be to [[Le Detachement De Gourmets#Members|Monsieur Mordre]]'s surprise. Seemingly attempting to recover from this apparent mistake, he decides to avoid physical battle in favor of a culinary challenge. 
- Suggesting that he also has significant ties to the [[Raven Queen]], he divulges that [[Le Detachement De Gourmets]] as a group have a pact with her. After intentionally disrespecting and embarrassing [[Madame Wonder]], [[Le Detachement De Gourmets#Members|Monsieur Mordre]] calls upon her to arrive and judge their cooking contest.
- The [[Raven Queen]]'s true form is shown (available in her article) and she sends each party into a "labyrinth of choices", but not before returning the [[Phandalin Fellows]] gear to them.
	- Upon hearing that [[Le Detachement De Gourmets#Members|Monsieur Mordre]] imprisoned a "Taelsin", she chops his arm off before summoning the [[Blade of Chained Shadows]] and returning it to [[Madame Wonder]].
- Puzzles. Cooking. Tomfoolery.
- Raven Queen discardes [[Le Detachement De Gourmets]], stating that she only ever asked them to produce a dish she could taste and that they spend hundreds of years failing to do so.
- Returning to the [[Material Plane]], [[Sildar Hallwinter]] meets them in his full armor for the first time ever. Cautiously walking up, he threatens Archibald by stating that he just left the "real" [[Archibald]] at the [[Stonehill Inn & Tavern]]. Upon seeing this false "Archibald" (known now as [[Anti-Bald]]) unable to think of an excuse, he attacks.
- After a brief combat encounter, in which everyone but [[Sildar Hallwinter]] was effectively frightened or incapacitated, [[MGs]] casts Enlarge on [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] to bring him to a towering ten feet tall.
- As [[Anti-Bald]] seems to be defeated he offers words of warning to the entire party, as written in the [[Session 10 - Cooking With Shar#Anti-Bald's Warning|next section]]. 
- [[Archibald]], [[Doormat]], [[Droop]], and [[Mister Alderleaf]] arrive shortly thereafter, and [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] reminds them that today is Midsummer Night's Eve. Tonight is the night; the expedition of [[Wave Echo Cave]] is a mere few hours away. 
- As [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] and the [[Phandalin Fellows]] are returning to town, [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] informs them that the [[Lord's Alliance]] has pulled many strings to attract merchants and tourists from all over the [[Sword Coast]]. He advises them to enjoy the festivities while they can, and to meet  [[Gundren Rockseeker]] and himself at the ruins of the [[Tresendar Manor]] just before sunset.
