### Session Short Overview
- After a daring escape from the castle led by an adrenaline-boosted [[Droop]], the party regroups and makes distance as fast as they can
- After brief rest and difficult conversation, they decide to make way for the rumored orc encampment followed by their quest to find [[Agatha]].
- Sneaking towards the camp, the party immediately notices that all the orcs stationed as lookouts are unarmed, and some injured.
	- Seeing this, [[Archibald]] takes the lead and instructs the others to follow his example. He justifies this by stating previously unknown information about his life, including his forceful exile from noblehood, supposedly due to the grandeur and expense of his parties. Yet, his time as a noble of the [[Fleetwood Family]] served him well, as some debt his family owes to orckind decades ago.
- Introducing himself and the party in Orcish, the lookouts call to an orc named [[Jukha]]. A finely toned yet older figure arises, and greets the party in Common. After brief conversation with him, the party is invited to share a moment of respite inside the temporary encampment, where [[Jukha]] begins unravelling the shrouded mystery of [[Wave Echo Cave]], or at least of what the [[Phandalin Fellows|Fellows]] knows thus far. In turn, he introduces himself to each party member in their native tongues, establishing a level of intelligence uncommon amongst mortal beings.
- As the party has known thus far, the [[Forge of Spells]] was created when humans, halflings, gnomes, and dwarves stumbled upon the base of the [[Sword Mountains]] and discovered a mysterious cave brimming with untapped magical energy. Seeking to harness this energy, they made the [[Phandelver's Pact]] to spread the wealth and resources amongst [[Phandalin|Phandalinites]] and establish a great city. However, one day this wondrous well of hidden strength was attacked by cultists and an accompanying orc army. [[Jukha]] retells this story, yet finalizes by framing it as the perspective held by time; and as he reminds the party, truth is often muddled by time. 
- It is at that point that [[Jukha]] tells them the truth; or as he puts it, the perspective held by orcs. The spring of magical energy was not a lucky break for the humanoids; it was a holy site for orcs, an ancient burial ground that the nomadic tribes from all over Fae'run made pilgrimage to at least once every generation. Jukha's ancestors were the tribe tasked with overseeing the [[Sword Mountains]] and surrounding areas, as far north as [[Neverwinter]] and south as [[Waterdeep]]. As he recalls it, the intruders were unaware of their transgressions, yet their ingrained biases against orckind prevented any diplomatic relations from being established. As the tribe began to split, there were two somewhat distinct sides that bickered amongst themselves: those who sought to seek peaceful resolution through an inclusion in [[Phandelver's Pact]] despite the tampering of their holy site, and those who believed that only force could put an end to the abuse that all of orckind has endured that culminated with their deceased being unable to rest. (HIS FULL MONOLOGUE IS POSTED BELOW THE SHORT SUMMARY SECTION)
- However, before either side reached the point of taking action without leaving room for discussion, cultists descended upon [[Wave Echo Cave]] with an army of unfamiliar orcs and attacked without reason. [[Jukha]]'s ancestors, regardless of what side they were on previously, attempted to prevent the total destruction of their ancient grounds and defend [[Wave Echo Cave]] along with the humans, dwarves, halflings, and gnomes of [[Phandalin]]. However, none of the [[Phandalin|Phandalinites]] could recognize this united goal, and slaughtered the orcs indiscriminately. The cave entrance collapsed in the war, and the remaining numbers of [[Jukha]]'s ancestors fled to continue living. The cultists fled, yet throughout the generations no one, orc or otherwise, were able to figure out who they really were. 
- [[Jukha]] allows the party to ask any additional questions they wish, yet leaves them with only one request. If they truly seek to recover [[Wave Echo Cave]], they must respect the orc's wishes and find a solution that does not anger the spirits within. Those who were disrespected even decades after their passing to the [[City of Judgement]] deserve to not be trifled with any longer. [[Jukha]] states that he will meet them at the entrance on Midsummer Night's Eve, the only day of year that the spirits allow mortals to enter without incurring the wrath of generations.
- As the party finishes their conversation, Jukha notes that [[Gundren Rockseeker]] is their technical benefactor, and urges them to bring him to [[Wave Echo Cave]] when they arrive for a peaceful negotiation of the lands. He also informs them that their only supporter is a dragonborn barkeep that the party has become quite familiar with: [[Gunther]]. Finally, [[Archibald]] asks if his tribe has any history on the [[Fleetwood Family]]; to which [[Jukha]] shares a surprised look. He states that the history of the [[Fleetwood Family]] is thanks to his ancestors, but he will only share those secrets at the location they originated from.
- As the party leaves, argument ensues. [[Fry]] is not trustful of the orc's story, citing its convenience as reason to question its validity. On the contrary, [[Archibald]] fully sides with the orcs, holding full belief of their perspective through his own history. [[Madame Wonder]] and [[Shayla]] remain somewhat neutral, holding doubts about both perspectives. Befuddled and a step away from conflict, the party heads to [[Agatha]]'s lair to complete their last assignment before returning to [[Phandalin]].
- [[Gundren Rockseeker]] is awake when they return to the carriage they left [[Droop]] and [[Doormat]], and after brief reintroductions they send him on his way with one of their horses to reach [[Phandalin]] and regroup with [[Sildar Hallwinter]] and to hopefully find his lost brothers.
- After a half-day's travel, the party reaches the ruins of Thundertree, where [[Agatha]] the banshee is said to hold her only residence on the mortal plane. They see a small pond, glimmering magically even amongst the golden glow of the sunset. Knit branches from the surrounding drooping willow trees sits over this pond, webbed together into a sort of small treehouse. A dim candlelight emanates from the openings between branches, yet none of the party can see inside.
- After a brief split in ideals, the entire party finds their way inside with Madame Wonder holding the jeweled silver comb given to them by [[Sister Garaele]]. However, remembering the words of the elf priestess, she hesitates. Seemingly tempted to ask about the many mysteries swirling in their fate, she brushes the doubt aside and calls for [[Agatha]].
- A swirling figure made of glowing spores and magical particles forms in front of them, as the candlelight flickers and dims. A tall elven woman materializes as the air inside the shack reaches near the point of frostbite. [[Agatha]] has revealed herself, or at least her chosen form.
- After careful negotiation and polite exchanges, [[Agatha]] agrees to answer any singular question in exchange for the comb. The party collectively hesitates, before they unanimously agree to ask for the location of Bowgentle's spellbook. Despite her best efforts to tempt each member into asking a different question, [[Agatha]] reluctantly informs them that she gave the spell book to a crazed wizard named [[Quixon]] several decades back and has no idea if he's even still alive, much less his or the spellbooks' whereabouts.
- As the party turns to leave, [[Agatha]] offers a fortune telling for each member for no apparent price. Everyone but [[Shayla]], who is very distrustful of magic and those who wield it, opts in for a fortune telling. Unknown to them, the fortunes are read aloud for all present members to hear, and [[Shayla]] recieves a note with hers on it, despite refusing. ### Jukha's Proposal
Our ancestor's transgressions are not indicative of their own free will, and even their ancestors were misunderstood. Wave Echo Cave is a holy site to orckind, and an ancestral burial ground of the orc population on the Sword Coast. Many orcs make a pilgrimage to it at some point in their lives. However, when the humans, gnomes, and dwarves stumbled upon it, the strong, latent magical power was all they understood. As they began to harness it for the production of magical tools and weapons, our ancestors saw it as tampering with our theology and ancestry. Conversation on how to remedy this issue caused a divide amongst the orcs, with an aggressive militaristic force and an anti-violent community. However, it is the militaristic side that is remembered in your history, I'm sure. 

We only seek now a place to call home. We caught wind of Cragmaw Castle and how it is hidden from your kinds, and thought it to be where our lineages may continue. However, the goblin infestation combined with the Black Spider's cunning defense is not something we can handle. We have no weapons, out of fear that holding any may be cause for action by adventurers such as yourselves. We seek only a place to build our community, free from the persecution of Phandalinites. 

We have little to offer besides ourselves. However, our help simply cannot come free due to our current livelihood being at stake. We have no need for coin; solely a place to nuture new life. If we allow this information to you, I ask on behalf of my people that you allow us to inhabit the real estate created by your employer's greed. Should we be able to get back on our feet and have less hardship to tether our people, I could rally many of our former warriors to aid in your future causes. What say you, adventurers of intellect and reason?


### Fortunes

#### Archibald
The Angel of the Seven Heavens is a mystery to most, his stories lost to time. I used to know him well, perhaps even enough to call him friend; yet the betrayal done to him by his kin has caused the Grandfather of Dragons to not leave his home in centuries. Your allies might seek those who forsake your oathgiver, despite his wishes for you. Just remember; when the mortal of your station forces the abuse of your own oath, you will have no option but to seek out the Draco Paladin. Remember that Mount Celestia is not a place to tread lightly, and despite appearances this mortal is not your enemy. Know this, if nothing else: the mortal's only difference to you is that his conquest was made black and white, while your strength comes from navigating the token slate gray of the Platinum Dragon.

#### Fry
The islands of your original home, in the truest sense. Yet, your lack of attachment to this place could stall your journey, as you must return to the forgotten archipelago. You are quite interesting, wise one. I cannot tell you much you do not already know: you are already acutely aware that you must return to make peace with the heretics of the Justicemaker. I also cannot break the forgotten contracts you made with your past self: all i can truly say is that your old friends yet live, despite the flames of your nightmares. They will come to you in decades time, and it is then you will find your true purpose in the presence of comrades old and new, a troupe forged of unlikely individuals. Know that these countrymen will remain loyal until your death. Wise one of the forge, your hammer could punch the very nail that saves this world, or seals the coffin of its conviction. Choose wisely.

#### Madame Wonder
I could speak for centuries on the futility of resisting any orders and contracts made from or with Lady Death. I can only offer a warning you should heed to the utmost: a great Domain of Dread is in your future, regardless of any possible preventions you may take. Resistance to this eventuality will lead to only to eventual madness, as sure as the oceans waves will erode rocky beaches. When the time has come, tides rich with jealousy will drag you and your compatriots to a demiplane of the Shadowfell to fight on the sky's floor. You can choose to either give up, or rekindle the humanity you believe to have lost throughout your troubled life. Just remember, child of ancient sin. Your greatest adversary is always your own shadow: beware the inversion.
