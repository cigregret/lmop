### Session Short Overview
- Began with entering the castle from the northern side, attempting to use stealth and a proposed "secret" entrance. 
	- [[Droop]] is sent to the carriage in hopes that he will bring it around, [[Doormat]] is armed with a javelin and remains with the party
- as the party enters the tower, they hear sounds of fighting coming from [[King Grol]]'s chamber. [[Archibald]] kicks the door down, and they see a multitude of figures already involved in battle
	- [[King Grol]] and his pet dire wolf, [[Aokiji]], an unconscious [[Gundren Rockseeker]], and a cloaked drow seeming to be the [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]]. 
- As the [[Phandalin Fellows]] join the fray, the [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]] rushes over to [[Gundren Rockseeker]] and begins to stab his already bloodied and mortally wounded body while fending off the attackers.
- Making swift work of [[King Grol]] and his pet dire wolf with their new abilities, the party enters a heated encounter with the [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]] as she is able to fend them all off while repeatedly slashing at [[Gundren Rockseeker]]'s wounded form.
	- During the peak of this encounter, [[Doormat]] is incapacitated and [[Gundren Rockseeker]] is repeatedly put on the verge of death, forcing [[Fry]] to work overtime.
- Eventually, she grabs what seems to be a map case and attaches it to one of her exploding daggers. Throwing it at [[Gundren Rockseeker]]'s body, she attempts a quick escape through the crowded room.
- After a brief scuffle in the hallway, the supposed [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]] is defeated and revealed to be a doppleganger beast, a highly intelligent creature that replicates the exact physical characteristics of any creature it has interacted with.
- As [[Madame Wonder]] sacrifices the soul of this foe to the [[Raven Queen]], [[Shayla]] intimidates some of the roaming hobgoblin guards into giving her an offering and to not disturb the chambers of [[King Grol]]for the forseeable future.
- They escape the castle the same way they came in, with a stabilized [[Gundren Rockseeker]] and [[Doormat]], sending [[Shayla]] to find [[Droop]] and the carriage.
	- She finds [[Droop]] at the carriage, at which point he explains that the carriage cannot be taken through the thick woods and the only way to get everyone on board would be to either dash across the open field that [[Cragmaw Castle]] sits on, or make the same maneuver with the carriage.
- This leaves the party somewhat split, with two injured compatriots, and an inability to quickly escape.
