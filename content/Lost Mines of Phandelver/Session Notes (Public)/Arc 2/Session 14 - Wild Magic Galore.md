### Short Summary
- Returning to the [[Feywilde]] from the [[Astral Plane]] as per [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools]]' power, the [[Phandalin Fellows]], [[Sildar Hallwinter]], and an unconscious [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]] find themselves in the [[Arena of Wild Magic]]. [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools]] greets them with a warm welcome, and return for [[MGs]] and [[Madame Wonder]].
	- "WEEEEEEELLLCOMEEEEE TOOOOOOO THE ONEEEE THE ONLYYYYY ARENAAAAAAA OF WILD MAAAAAGIIIIIIIIIIIIIIC!!!! THE UNDEFEATED CHAMPIONS HAVE RETURNED FOR ROUUUUUUUUND TWOOOOOOOO!!!"
- The crowd, going ballistic, consists of millions of drunk fey cheering and frothing at the mouth for a bloody gladiator show. As the crowd is hyped up, [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools]] approaches the party and immediately puts [[MGs]] to sleep, picking up both him and [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]]. Stating that they have both fulfilled their purpose and returned home, he begins to walk away.
- However, before he can continue the show [[Madame Wonder]] taps into her inner gladiator and screams to the crowd for true stakes to be put on the match; either eternal servitude to the [[Arena of Wild Magic]], or the freedom of their entire party and safe return to the [[Material Plane]]. [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools|Cerise]], whether pressured by the crowd or simply amused at the sentiment, agrees to the terms and states that he will summon four of the party's allies to help them in this battle. However, they must kill every creature on the field themselves otherwise they will be imprisoned forever.
	- The three other teams consist of the Bloody Reds, Blue Lightning, and the Knights of the Court (eight Redcaps, twelve Quicklings, and two Mantis Knights with their pet Displacer Beasts, respectively).
- [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools|Cerise]] takes his leave, bringing the bodies of [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]] and [[MGs]] and the two [[Three Great Relics|Great Relics]] that the party had in their possession. The party's allies begin to appear, and their mere presence brings the group's morale up tenfold. As [[Doormat]], [[Droop]], and [[Jukha]] are magically summoned, the party rejoices. However, after a brief reunion, they realize that their three allies seem significantly older.
- The [[Fellas of the Fellows]], as they claim themselves, begin to explain that it has been forty years since the party entered [[Wave Echo Cave]] and [[Gundren Rockseeker]] has swept into massive political and economic power from the monopoly of magical items produced through the [[Forge of Spells]]. Before they can explain further though, the fourth ally is summoned.
- Built up from a signature puddle of purple goo, the reunited party looks on horrified as the slime builds into a familiar, yet sinister figure: [[Anti-Bald]], the doppelgänger. As the party begins to react, [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools|Cerise]] calls for the match to begin. The wheel is spun and everyone is immediately levitated twenty feet into the air.
- After numerous close calls and a bloody battle of attrition later, the party succeeds in single-handedly killing every enemy and fulfilling their end of the contract. Congratulated by [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools|Cerise]] and celebrated by the millions of fey, they are brought up to [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools|Cerise's]] home in the sky, far above the [[Arena of Wild Magic|Arena]]. There sits an awakened [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]], as well as a seemingly asleep [[MGs]]. [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools]] greets them again, thanking the [[Phandalin Fellows]] for the show. [[Anti-Bald]] attempts to kill [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools|Cerise]], who meets him with only a small wave as if swatting a fly. [[Anti-Bald]]'s body begins to melt yet again, as he curses the party stating that "the new dawn is soon upon the [[Material Plane]], and her goal will be achieved."
- As they inquire about [[MGs]]' state, [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools|Cerise]] simply smiles and waves his hand. To the party's horror, the body of their druid begins to melt into a puddle of blue goo almost identical except in color to how [[Anti-Bald]] was just dismissed. As they stare at the puddle, a knock is heard on the door of this eloquent drawing room. As heads turn, the one who opens the door is none other than [[MGs]], whose face seems slightly older, yet kinder.
