### Background
More than five hundred years ago, clans of dwarves and gnomes made an agreement known as the [[Phandelver's Pact]], by which they would share a rich mine in a wondrous cavern known as [[Wave Echo Cave]]. In addition to its mineral wealth, the mine contained great magical power. Human spellcasters allied themselves with the dwarves and gnomes to channel and bind that energy into a great forge (called the [[Forge of Spells]]), where magic items could be crafted. Times were good, and the nearby human town of [[Phandalin]] (pronounced fan-duh-lin) prospered as well. But then disaster struck when orcs swept through the North and laid waste to all in their path.

A powerful force of orcs reinforced by evil mercenary wizards attacked [[Wave Echo Cave]] to seize its riches and magic treasures. Human wizards fought alongside their dwarf and gnome allies to defend the [[Forge of Spells]], and the ensuing spell battle destroyed much of the cavern. Few survived the cave-ins and tremors, and the location of [[Wave Echo Cave]] was lost.

For centuries, rumors of buried riches have attracted treasure seekers and opportunists to the area around [[Phandalin]], but no one has ever succeeded in locating the lost mine. In recent years, people have resettled the area. [[Phandalin]] is now a rough-and-tumble frontier town. More important, the [[Rockseeker Brothers|Rockseeker brothers]] have discovered the entrance to [[Wave Echo Cave]], and they intend to reopen the mines.

Unfortunately for the [[Rockseeker Brothers|Rockseekers]], they are not the only ones interested in [[Wave Echo Cave]]. A mysterious villain known as the [[Black Spider]] controls a network of bandit gangs and goblin tribes in the area, and their agents have followed the [[Rockseeker Brothers|Rockseekers]] to their prize. Now the [[Black Spider]] wants [[Wave Echo Cave]] for themself, and they are taking steps to make sure no one else knows where it is.



### Session Detailed Overview
[[Archibald]], [[Fry]], and [[Madame Wonder]] are delivering supplies to [[Phandalin]] for [[Gundren Rockseeker]] and [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] They have been traveling for several weeks now, as they were coming from [[Baldur's Gate]]. About a half-day's journey from [[Phandalin]], they stop on the [[Triboar Trail]] to investigate two dead horses. As [[Archibald]] steps out of the carriage to investigate, the trio are ambushed by four goblins hiding alongside the trail. A battle ensues, which the trio swiftly kills three of the goblins present.

As the fourth attempts to escape, [[Archibald]] takes a length of rope from [[Madame Wonder]], grapples the goblin, and ties it to the carriage's wheel. Then, in an odd move of interrogation, he pulls out his [[Dragonchess]] set and teaches the goblin how to play with [[Fry]]'s help. Eventually, [[Archibald]] calmly questions the goblin while playing chess, taking note of the goblin's innate skill at the game. [[Fry]] and [[Archibald]] learn that a dwarf was kidnapped recently and taken to [[Cragmaw Castle]], yet this goblin knows not of where the castle is. However, the goblin does share that it did come from a hideout where the human who was with the dwarf is currently being kept, and will show the way in exchange for its freedom. As this conversation happens, [[Madame Wonder]] investigates the dead horses. Her keen eye notices that one horse belonged to [[Gundren Rockseeker]], the party's employer, and had an an empty map satchel attached to the saddle. 

The party then tie the goblin up inside the carriage, knock him out, and make their way into town. The goblin, given severe blunt force trauma, relearns his name as [[Doormat]] and enters the employ of [[Archibald]] as a squire. However, the party failed to consider what the townspeople would think of a live goblin being brought into the main square. As [[Madame Wonder]] attempted to convince the crowd that a performance involving the goblin would take place later that night, an older human man named [[Toblen Stonehill]] walks up to [[Fry]] and asks if [[Gundren Rockseeker]] sent them. 

[[Toblen Stonehill]] then introduced himself as owner of the [[Stonehill Inn & Tavern]], and offered to direct them towards the building and set them up with accommodations. He also offered to take them around town and introduce them to the local businesses so that they may become acquainted with their surroundings while under the employ of [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] While driving towards the [[Stonehill Inn & Tavern]], [[Fry]] offered [[Toblen Stonehill]] a hit of what he claimed was 'mid pack', but what was in fact salvia. [[Toblen Stonehill]], being a slightly older human, instantly crumpled to the ground upon being convinced to take a hit.

The party then has to explain why a pillar of the community that runs a family business of multiple generations is frothing at the mouth and unconscious on the street. As [[Madame Wonder]] and [[Archibald]] are trying to get them out of the situation, [[Fry]] spots four individuals clad in red leather armor darting through the crowd, seeming to be pickpockets of a sort. Eventually the party gets [[Toblen Stonehill]] back into the carriage and makes it to the alley between the [[Stonehill Inn & Tavern]] and [[Barthen's Provisions]]. The party pays for two nights' stay, then splits up to deliver the goods to [[Elmar Barthen]] and convince the bartender of [[Stonehill Inn & Tavern]], a brass dragonborn names [[Gunther]], to allow a chessboxing spectacle at the tavern tonight.

Through much confusion, the party succeeds in getting paid for their successful delivery and convincing [[Gunther]] to allow [[Doormat]] to chessbox on the tavern's stage. While waiting for the tavern to grow busy, [[Gunther]] requests a chessboxing match against [[Doormat]]. [[Doormat]] has the upper hand in chess, but everyone knows that the burly dragonborn would murder [[Doormat]] as soon as the boxing started. However, to everyone's surprise, [[Doormat]] was able to land a cheap shot on [[Gunther]]'s balls to win the match. When [[Gunther]] returns behind the bar, the party decides that their time is best spent playing a drinking game instead of visiting literally anywhere around town, and thus [[Gunther]] pours fresh mugs of [[Dwarves' Blood]], a potent crimson mead. 

[[Archibald]] and [[Madame Wonder]] have several glasses each, both attempting to outdrink the other. However, all this ends up doing is pushing them both close to blackout, and as the tavern grows busier they can only hope to execute their chessboxing hustle on the townspeople. Thanks to [[Fry]]'s dwarven constitution, they have little trouble downing several glasses and continuing about the party's plan. However, after they have swindled a young gnome and elderly human, one of the figured clad in red armor steps up to the stage. [[Fry]], being the only one with any suspicion as to what could be going on, begins to signal to the party that its time to cut their losses. [[Madame Wonder]] and [[Archibald]] are too drunk to notice. 

The mysterious bald man puts down a hefty sum of 35 gold, and begins to play. [[Doormat]] seems anxious and like he has to try his absolute hardest just to keep up with the man. At this point, [[Archibald]] notices three more individuals in the crowd with matching leather armor, using the distraction of the chessboxing to pickpocket the tavern-goers. He signals to [[Madame Wonder]], who finally gets the message and casts a spell to make their lookout scream in abject horror. The ones in the crowd sprint for the exit and the one on stage makes a blunder so obvious it had to be intentional, before calmly walking out of the tavern. [[Madame Wonder]] tries to follow, but once she gets outside the four have fully vanished. 

As the energy and population in the tavern starts to dwindle in the late hours of the night, [[Fry]] asks [[Gunther]] if he knew who any of those men were. [[Gunther]] then explains to the party that [[Phandalin]] has a problem with a human gang known as the [[Redbrands]]. They rob local businesses, kidnap children, and yet have remained unchecked by the townmaster, [[Harbin Wester]]. [[Gunther]] states that they frequent an old abandoned building on the east side of town, once called the [[Sleeping Giant Tavern]]. [[Fry]] thanks [[Gunther]] for the information, and the party goes upstairs to bed.
