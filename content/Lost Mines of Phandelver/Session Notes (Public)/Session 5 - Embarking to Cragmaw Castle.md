### Session Detailed Overview
As the party returned to the [[Stonehill Inn & Tavern]], [[Fry]] spotted a mysterious shadow standing on the top of the tavern while the others were focused on other things. This figure, clad in draped black cloth, pointed directly at [[Fry]] then disappeared. [[Archibald]] decided to more closely investigate [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar's]] recent acquisition of what appears to be his standard armor and weaponry, noticing a sigil of a Paladin's Oath. Upon recognizing it, he grew even more restless and anxious about [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar's]] supposed allegiance with the party.

As they entered the tavern, [[Gunther]] requested to hear about the fruits of their travels, to which [[Madame Wonder]] retold a descriptive performance; notably excluding the two near death experiences she experienced. [[Droop]] managed to snag some more coin for [[Shayla]], who was also a bit shook by her mentor's recent actions and attitude. 

As the story was finished, the tavern erupted into cheers upon hearing of the [[Redbrands|Redbrands']] swift defeat and expulsion from [[Phandalin]]. Requesting a party name to chant, some deliberation ensued. [[Gunther]] ended up appropriating [[Madame Wonder|Madame's]] idea, leading to our party now being known as the [[Phandalin Fellows]]. This ragtag misfit group brought together initially by binding contracts to complete simple work in this land had now led to public popularity and feats under their belt. Before our heroes took to their rooms, they conferred with [[Sildar Hallwinter]] on the next best steps to take. He urged them to prioritize rescuing [[Gundren Rockseeker]], the employer of [[Fry]], [[Archibald]], and [[Madame Wonder]], as well as a close ally of [[Sildar Hallwinter]] and [[Shayla]] through the [[Lord's Alliance]]. They resolved to gear up in the morning.

As the night grew late, our wounded heroes returned to their quarters for much needed rest. However, they grew from the experiences they have had thus far, unlocking new potential within themselves. This was realized in the forms of unique dreams, whether that be divine insight, mysterious messages, or reliving past traumas to reclaim their histories. Each persons' dreams are documented below, along with the accompanying music!


#### Archibald
Song(s): 
Mohg, Lord of Blood - Elden Ring Official Soundtrack - Shoi Miyazawa

You see visions of battle. Not any war that you have heard of, or a fight you have been part of, but witness indiscernable figures cutting each other down. You feel the valor, the adrenaline, the cravings of dominance. This carnal desire is to seek glory in the subjugation of your enemies. You have felt it before, even stronger every time you have cast your divine smite. Yet now, it is intoxicating and you are lulled into the pleasures of war. Bearing witness to this unquelled urge for carnage, you scream a warcry of pure valor, joining the fray. 

Dashing and slashing your newfound greatsword as if you have been arcanely attuned to it for years, dominating these foes without compromising your confidence. Your refusal to dodge would typically spell suicide, but the pure refusal to bend your unstoppable will has formed into an aura that weakens those around you. Without missing a beat, you begin to admire your intensity. Your conviction. Your subjection. And as the intoxication of this eternal conquest manifests wihin every action you make, a voice with no source rings in your ears.

"Douse the flame of hope. Tis ne'er enough to merely defeat thine enemy in battle; thine victory must be so overwhelming, thine foes' will to fight is shattered forever. A blade can end a life, but fear can topple an empire."

You feel a searing pain on your bicep, yet continue to slash and smite every creature you witness.

"Rule with an iron fist. Once thy hast conquered, tolerate no dissent. Thine word is law. Those whom obey it shall be favored. Those who defy it shall be punished, as an example to all whom might course."

The searing pain gets hotter, as if an open flame lays upon it, yet your muscle refuses to lose strength while your eyes view more adversaries.

"Strength above all. Thou shall rule whislt no stronger arises. If a stronger is arisen, thy might not but grow mightier to meet thine challenge, or fall to thy own wrack."

It feels as if your arm is being branded, as the flames lick your skin. Your aura exudes such a fulfilling sustenance that you feel as if you could survive on it alone.

"Long and hard didst thou fight. Now off, tyrant of knights, iron monger of dragon. Aye, thy grim oath hath been sworn of conquest. Now off, 'i mine name as ye Angel of the Seven Heavens, ye Justicemaker; Bahamut, ye God of All Dragons."

You awaken. Checking your right bicep that felt such intense flame, you see the three tenets of your oath seared into your arm:
	- Douse the flame of hope
	- Rule with an iron fist
	- Strength above all
This dogma glows a warm crimson, as if branded onto you by the fire of a dragon.

#### Madame Wonder
Song(s): 
Eternal City - Elden Ring Official Soundtrack - Yuka Kitamura

As you drift to sleep, you reminisce the nothic you encountered just hours prior. The nothic's probing into your mind, your past, your secrets, your murders, your loss, all of it overwhelms you in your sleep. That devilish Infernal seeps into your mind. The whispers grow louder.

In the battle with Mosk the bugbear, you reached closer to death than you ever had before, seemingly only needing to reach our her hand to embrace it. The whispers grow louder.

Incapacitated now not by sleep but by the overwhelming pressure of the nothic, the experience of touching Lady Death's fingertips, the cacophony of unbridled screams ringing in your mind, you begin to give up all hope of continuing. Then, a voice resonates in her mind. Yet this voice was not the harsh Infernal speak of a fiend, but was speaking in a gentle, soothing Common. 

"My shadowborne, you have endured enough. Even existing on the Material Plane, free of fey light and the mirrors of dark, should not mean you are forsaken of any solace...

Take my hand, my shadowborne. You have birthed yourself anew as Madame Wonder, yet the Nine Hells have refused to allot that respect. Take my hand and be free of the Infernal hatred, live as you were born to. Take my hand, my gladiator."

You reach for the outstretched slender fingers that have no wrist nor arm. As you grasped the fingertips of this voice, the Infernal screams dissipate instantly, replaced by something you have never heard: silence. Then the voice speaks again:

"I will call on you when you are most needed, and will not sully your pain with meaningless tasks. Now rise, my shadowborne. Enter battle for the Raven Queen, bearing the title of her esteemed gladiator."

As you awaken, you find an unfamiliar weapon in your hand, yet you're holding it tightly. The voice speaks once more, as if naming the weapon:

"The Blade of Chained Shadows"


#### Shayla
Song(s):
Lake (From "Pokemon Diamond and Pearl") - Pokemon Diamond and Pearl Chiptunes - Chippy Bits
A Place by a Lake - Omori Official Soundtrack - Omori

The wind blows your unhooded hair as the gentle sea spray mists your face. You stand in the crow's nest, with nothing but vast aquamarine ocean covering all you can see. Even the sky seems to mirror this beauty as the sun rises, and gentle blues streak across the horizon. The boards of this ship are still wet from last night's storm, yet the air is filled with more than the smell of fresh rainfall. There is a feeling of anticipation from all members of this ship in the Moonshae Mariner's fleet; the ship tha is set to reopen the borders of the Moonshae Isles to the outer world of Fae'run. 

By some miracle, you wound up on this ship that would make history once landfall was made. Especially being the sole woman in a mariner's crew, working the crow's nest no less. And that moment of history wouldn't come too long from now; the city of Waterdeep, your destination, appeared on the horizon hours ago. Thus, a five-week-long journey is nearly over.

As the ship reaches the dock, your instincts begin to falter. Detecting some form of threat, you begin to nervously check your surroundings. Head on a swivel, you barely notice the ballistae of the port's guard towers are aimed directly at the ship already docking. However, before you have a chance to react the ballista fire, their bolts tearing through the hull and instantly crippling any chance of escape. Your crewmates scream and instincively jump to the dock, already devolving into an "every man for themself" mentality. You begin to climb down from the mast as fast as possible to get to safety. 

As you reach the upper deck, you turn towards the crew and see your quartermaster fall. Four different arrows fletched with black feathers protrude from his head and chest. Stepping towards the edge of the ship, these arrows of death strike your helmsmaster. Throwing yourself over to the dock, the cook falls. Then the navigator. The doctor. The first mate. The captain. All these people your teenage self has grown close to across the last month murdered within moments, without so much as a warning. Unable to even complete their goal of touching new soil, only getting so far as the manmade wooden catwalks of the Waterdeep Docks. They all fall, and you remain. Silently standing in the middle of the bodies.

Numb from shock, a voice scares you into consciousness. 
	"You. Name." 
A tall, young, darkskinned, half-orc walks towards you, with an olive-toned human man behind her. Still taken aback at the events, you do not answer. The woman walks within arm reach, then asks again: 
	"I said, what is your name, child."
As you open your mouth, her burly arm smacks it shut. 
	"You do not answer unless I tell you to, and you do it quickly." 
Gesturing to the human behind her, she says:
	"This is Sildar. He will be taking care of you from now on. You are nobody, a noone. You have no identity, no records, no name. You will work for me, or you will die."
She promptly turns and begins walking away, but the human she introduced hangs behind. Almost as if feeling your inquiries, she stops and continues to speak:
	"Do not worry; Sildar will not touch you unless it is with his blade. I hope you don't cut easily, as I cannot do much with a paper crane for my spy."
Chuckling to herself, she briefly looks down at the dead bodies of your crew.
	"I hope you did not like any of these men; they were all much too ugly for you. If you prove yourself sufficient, in time I will allow you to have a name; for now, follow Sildar's training and do exactly as I say."



#### Fry
Song(s):
Mountain Roads - Mountain Roads - ASKII
Zurawie (Maine death song) - Ugory

Sweat drips down your neck, shoulders, and back as you swing your arm from the canopy to the earth. The smell of crisp, spring air hits the back of your nostrils as you slam the finely carved arcane hammer into your wooden anvil. No sparks fly, but glimmers of the Weave shine against the late afternoon sunlight. As you grow weary, you decide that a break is in order for the sake of your creations and their quality. 

As you step away from the forge, you take note of the forest you've lived in for decades now; amber sunglight slipping through the soft green hues of spring are familiar, yet do not lose their charm. Your cycle of work has become habitual, as you make your way to the same log you've taken your smoke breaks on for the past forty years. You sit, smoking your pipe, deeply inhaling as you admire the crystaline butterflies dance through the waves of gentle breeze that push the grass opposite of its growth.

A childish scream finds its way to your ears, its source hiding within the trees. The druid children playing, most likely. While the circle does not come to your seclude often, the pact you forged upon your arrivals means you may rest easy under their protection. You continue to puff your pipe. The scent of smoke grows stronger as you pack more homegrown into the orifice.

Your eyes take inventory of the workspace you've created here; a humble abode, akin to a traveling monk who seeks to cultivate their soul's potential. A bedroll and tent, made almost entirely of the native flora and infused with druidic conjury to withstand the erosion brought by time. A small pantry comprised of jars with dried meats and herbs, yet no stove to practice the culinary arts with your procured ingredients. Another puff, and deep inhale to follow, the scent of smoke filling your throat and lungs. In the center of this natural clearing, sits a wooden anvil engraved with runes and surrounded by a soft whirlwind of bioluminescent spores. 

The grass around it is unnaturally well-kept, and, save the engravings, the anvil has no visible wear. Despite what youa assume to be the clouds blocking the sunlight from this centerpiece, you are reminded of your history with this anvil. You have crafted natural items with arcane capacity for as long as you've lived within this forest. This anvil has been more consistently used than many smithies of established cities, rivaling those tasked with military production. As you puff again, and the smoke hits your nostrils, you feel almost jolted back to conciousness. 

The smoke you inhaled was not from your pipe: its been empty for a number of minutes now. Standing up, it is now you notice that there were no clouds blocking the sunlight; a plume of thick, black smog has covered the natural sky. The forest is burning. The druids are burning. Your life is burning.

Time seems to warp around you as the trees quickly fall to ash and create dunes of black sand at your feet. The fire sweeps through the underbrush and canopy with ease, spreading like the plague at the Midsummer's Eve celebrations in Baldur's Gate. Nothing is left untouched, yet you cannot move within this dreamlike state. As your vision spins, the flames reach higher, and the heat feels as if it is forging your dwarven body into its original calloused state. 

Jolting awake, you reflect upon the memories you relived, the decades spent hammering tools with nature's favor and imbuing them with arcane secrets. This century of overwhelming knowledge has flooded back into your consciousness. The roaring inferno called to you with the same intent that you held when smithing equipment of the mystical arts; desiring nothing  how more than to forge you into the calloused shell you reside within now.




After ALL of that reading and fun little roleplay, everyone was awake as [[Sildar Hallwinter]] knocked on their doors and requested them to start getting ready for the journey ahead. Giving them a prepaid to-go breakfast from [[Gunther]], he guided the [[Phandalin Fellows]] to make an offering at the [[Shrine of Luck]], then to [[Denrar Smithy & Jeweler]] to sell precious stones and shit and buy equipment before leaving to go to [[Cragmaw Castle]].


#### Shrine of Luck

As you all walk across Phandalin's main square, you notice that the hum of daily life is stronger today. Glancing around, you see smiles on more people's faces, perhaps due to the news spreading of the Redbrand's defeat. As you approach the Shrine of Luck, a small cistern glittering with copper and silver coins, you notice that Sister Garaele is talking with someone.


[[Sister Garaele]] congratulated the party on their subjugation of the [[Redbrands]], and asked the party if they were able to handle her request concerning [[Agatha]] and Bowgentle's legendary spellbook. The [[Phandalin Fellows]], perhaps lost in their exaustion and the extreme circumstances as of late, reminded the party of the jeweled comb in their possession and only asked for them to ask [[Agatha]] of the spellbook's location. She urged for them to use proper manners and remain extremely polite in [[Agatha]]'s presence for their safety, and promised several "strong" healing items in return as well as possible future requests. 

She also mentioned the ruins of a town called [[Thundertree]], an abandoned village in the [[Neverwinter Wood]]. There is said to be a wise druid in these ruins that guards much of the forest, and whom could prove useful to the party's larger goals.

#### Barthen's Provisions
Stopping by [[Barthen's Provisions]], the party then purchased the rations necessary for their journey and rented a carriage from [[Elmar Barthen]]. They then set off on their two and a half day journey to [[Cragmaw Castle]], directed by [[Droop]].


#### Neverwinter Wood & Cragmaw Castle
As the party made their journey past the sprawling meadows and flowering biomes surrounding the [[Sword Mountains]], they entered the [[Neverwinter Wood]] for the first time. Quickly, a plume of black smoke was noticed above the treetops. [[Droop]] surmised that this must be the local orc encampment, and the party chose to steer clear for now and continue along their path to [[Cragmaw Castle]] to hopefully rescue [[Gundren Rockseeker]] and find [[Wave Echo Cave]] once and for all. Their plans were to find [[Agatha]]'s domain, scout the orc encampment, and explore the ruins of [[Thundertree]] after dealing with the larger issue at hand. 

After about a half day's more travel and a brief run-in with an owlbear and her cub, the [[Phandalin Fellows]] finally spotted the looming ruins of a once great castle in a clearing amongst the trees. They had arrived at [[Cragmaw Castle]]. These ruins told a story of once impenetrable defenses that had been lost to time, now covered in goblin graffiti and mismatched cloths adorning the battlements. Bringing the carriage to a halt, they sent [[Shayla]] to stealthily scout the exterior and bring back the information necessary to formulate a great plan.

This session ended with [[Shayla]] successfully remaining hidden amongst the cover of the trees, and spotting numerous defenses littering the remaining towers. [[Droop]] informed them that [[King Grol]]'s chambers are in the northeastern tower, conveniently near a possible secret entrance [[Shayla]]. After deliberating on a plan, the [[Phandalin Fellows]] compromised on an attack from this supposed entrance, remaining as stealthy as possible despite [[Archibald]] and [[Fry]]'s heavier armor making that option risky. They left the cart and rotated themselves along the northern wall, taking a short rest to wait for nightfall. It is here that this portion ends, yet the next shall be filled with bloodshed.
