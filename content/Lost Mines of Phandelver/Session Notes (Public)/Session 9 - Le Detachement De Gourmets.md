### Short Summary
- find their way to alderleaf farms, owned by an elderly halfling named Mister Alderleaf. he is an apprehensive, bigoted man who is visibly uncomfortable with the diversity of the party, specifically madame wonder. microaggressions, such as referring to wonder as "hellspawn" and largely ignoring them if they talk. 
- Tensions high, Mr. Alderleaf calls out for his farmhand: [[Grok]]. He leads the party to the cows, who are jet black and breathing slowly, as if already dead. After some investigation, [[MGs]] and [[Madame Wonder]] found:
	- arcane residue in the form of wisps that curl off the cows and point off the property
	- a physical burrow under the fence that the cows could fit through, along with tracks that lead further away
- Following these trails, they found themselves nearby the [[Phandalin]] graveyard. A large weeping willow tree hangs over this side of the graveyard, and the tracks/wisps lead to its base. As they get closer, the light of day start to dim and the wisps, whether seen or unseen up until this point, are visible to all and congeal into a door.
	- These effects are reversed if an individual chooses to step back. 
- As [[Madame Wonder]] touches the door, a deadly cold envelopes around the whole party, casting a blanket of magical darkness in a 15 foot radius around them. they then feel their bodies begin to shift, yet not in a way that is entirely physical. 
	- [[MGs]] senses that they are planeshifting, and warns the party to be prepared. [[Madame Wonder]] senses the same, yet her warning is different; the [[Raven Queen]] approaches.
- Everyone sees the spectral hand of the [[Raven Queen]] reach out, undoubtedly reaching towards [[Madame Wonder]]. Then, she speaks to no one in particular, but her voice feels as if it whispers into everyone's ears simultaneously.
	- **"Do not accept any propositions with the heretic you are about to meet. He seeks nothing more than a satisfactory meal for him and his troupe. This posse of gourmets reside within my domain, yet refuse to acknowledge the contract of the land. Deal with them as you like, but should any one of you become theirs I will not save you. I give none of you assistance further; my gladiator is proving enough for my amusement. Accepting clauses from these animals will make you enemy of mine and hers."**
- As the smog begins to clear, you see a grand manor not even thirty paces in front of you. Large balconies adorn the second story, while ivory pillars contrast against the ash wood of the building itself. You see bushes, carved into shapes of various animals and even monsters, some of which look entirely unfamiliar to you all. As you try to focus your eyes on these bushes, you notice small patches of odd-looking flowers amongst the greenery. Feeling like your vision seems to still be hazy, you maybe blink or rub your eyes; however, you notice that without a doubt, the flowers are gray. As you resurvey your surroundings, you realize that the ash wood and ivory you saw earlier seems almost too muted, and the air begins to weigh heavy and cold upon your neck and shoulders. Looking around for any other indicators as to where you are, or what is happening to you, a voice speaks.
	- **"Well hello my little cuties... Welcome to the Manoir of Gourmets. My name is Monsieur Mordre. I am the leader, if you will, of Le Detachement de Gourmets."**
- Inviting them in, he requests that the party leave their weapons behind and get dressed for the masquerade ball. Before anyone can object or comment, their vision swirls and they are transported inside the manor to a dressing room.
- After a brief non-argument with [[Monsieur Mordre]], they change into the attire and leave their weapons. [[Shayla]] decides to stow two daggers on the sides of her waist in preparation for a possible conflict. 
	- [[Grok]] also opts to rip down a nearby curtain to use as a toga, whereas everyone else dons identical suits and dresses.
- Forgoing any exploration, the party immediately heads to enter the ballroom and join the fray. They see many partygoers, and [[MGs]] decides to try the deviled eggs that servers are wandering the room with. It was described as the most pleasurable and delectable thing he has ever tasted, unrivaled in its deliciousness. 
- Then, [[Le Detachement De Gourmets]] take the stage to announce the five-course meal that will be served this evening. As the first course is wheeled out, courtesy of [[Monseiur Jean]], [[Grok]] immediately notices that it is one of his cursed cows that never returned (Jebediah, specifically). Also, all of the seemingly normal partygoers begin foaming at the mouth and their physical movements become janky and off-balance. They swarm the cow, tearing at it with their hands and trampling each other with no mind paid to their own health or the safety of others.
	- Then the second course, served by [[Monseiur Gabriel]]: owlbear. The swarm repeats.
	- Then the third course, served by [[Monseiur Louis]]: hobgoblin. The swarm repeats.
	- Then the fourth course, served by [[Monseiur Antione]]: dragonborn. The swarm repeats.
- During the spectacle, [[MGs]] makes a Fey sigil out of the untouched silverware and requests an omen from beyond the known planes. The omen replies with one word: **Woe**. Not seeing an opportunity to state this fortune to the others, he remains silent yet worried. [[Shayla]], after seeing dragonborn be served and devoured, calls to [[Monsieur Mordre]] on stage and requests to use the bathroom. He smiles, obliges, and instructs the entire party to follow him up the stairs behind the stage.
- As the climb the flight of stairs and reach the floor above, they quickly notice that this is simply a small balcony overlooking the ballroom itself. There is barely enough space for the entire party to fit, and the other members of [[Le Detachement De Gourmets]] wait on the stairs to block any exit.
- [[Grok]], quick to the offensive, attempts to throw a punch but is magically blinded and stripped naked as [[Monsieur Mordre]] attempts to throw him off the balcony ledge. Once he finally is over the railing and hurtling towards the floor, [[Monsieur Mordre]] reminds the party that he has not served his dish yet.
	- **Whether or not you truly expected to make it out of this eventuality, please note that I would be disrespecting the culinary arts and my fellow countrymen if I were to not deliver a course. I simply choose to keep my meals as fresh as possible before they are served.**
- As the others descend on their own, they quickly work together to dispatch the entire fleet of thrall partygoers, as the vampiric fangs of [[Le Detachement De Gourmets]] peek from behind their smirking lips while they watch from the balcony. 
- As they finally banish the last thralls to the general [[Shadowfell]], [[Monsieur Mordre]] confidently states that he has one last trick up his sleeve, as this is his [[Domains of Dread|Domain of Dread]]. A royal purple summoning circle forms in the center of the room, and a strange goop begins forming a figure in front of the [[Phandalin Fellows]]. 
- First steel greaves, then the beginnings of a chainmail skirt. The tip of a greatsword begins to shape, building the sharpened blade as the goop climbs towards the ceiling. A large breastplate, with shoulders and arms holding the greatsword's hilt. As the neckline forms, a cape unfurls down the back of the impeccably polished armor. The cape, bearing an insignia of a small twig with three leaves, made way for the face and head of a familiar human...
