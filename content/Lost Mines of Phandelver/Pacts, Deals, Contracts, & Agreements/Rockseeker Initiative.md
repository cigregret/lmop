The coalition and action plan formed between the [[Lord's Alliance]] and [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] for the purpose of rediscovering and reopening the [[Forge of Spells]] within [[Wave Echo Cave]]. The motivations for each party are different, but the overarching public intention is to create economic prosperity within the central [[Sword Coast]] region around [[Phandalin]]. The [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] claim to have information on the whereabouts of [[Wave Echo Cave]], allowing a near monopoly of magical tools and weapons which would be exceptionally profitable and undoubtedly create thousands of jobs in the area. The **Rockseeker Initiative** was not made with input nor consent of any communities/cultures surrounding [[Wave Echo Cave]] and its history, including but not limited to: surviving orcs, [[Phandalin|Phandalinites]] (gnomes, dwarves, and humans), [[Cragmaw Tribe]], and the dragons of the [[Sword Mountains]].
