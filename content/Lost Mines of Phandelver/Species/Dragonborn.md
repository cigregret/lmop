Born of dragons, as their name proclaims, the dragonborn are humanoid dragons with height averages of about six foot and six inches. Originally shaped by draconic gods from the essence of the [[Elemental Chaos]] and lesser essence of the [[Astral Sea]], dragonborn combine the best attributes of dragons and humanoids and mainly reside now on the [[Material Plane]]. Dragonborn can be many different colors, all reminiscent of the known dragon types, yet the color of their scales bear little correlation to a dragonborn's draconic heritage.

Despite having no ancestral links to true dragons, the blood of particular dragon types ran in the different dragonborn clans, giving them the traits and some abilities of these types of dragons. Some dragonborn believed that they had the blood of the dragon gods Bahamut, Tiamat, and Sardior, instead. There were three known draconic ancestries: the more common chromatic and metallic ancestries, and the rarer gem ancestry.
