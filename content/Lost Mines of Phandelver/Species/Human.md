The most common species on the [[Material Plane]], **humans** are found nearly everywhere one could look. They also tend to find themselves wherever one strikes its fancy. They aren't particularly known for anything, nor does their construction offer any one large distinguishing feature. Additionally, unlike other species, humans are one of the only creatures in existence that have no deity of origin. They seemingly just appeared millennia ago, and no accessible records explain this phenomenon.
