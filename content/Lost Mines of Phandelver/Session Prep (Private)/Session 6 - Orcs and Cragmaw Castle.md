### Orc Encampment
start with the travel from agatha to the orcs, slowly through the neverwinter woods. when they find the orcs, let them clearly spot their numbers (12 excluding guards) and the two standing guard. decent perception rolls show that they are injured, unarmed, and cooking a meal. 

if the party decides to walk up and talk, make them calm, educated, and intelligent. their leader, [[Jukha]], knows several languages and will communicate with them to the respective party member:
- [[Archibald]] - common
- [[MGs]]/[[Fry]] - dwarvish
- [[Madame Wonder]] - 
- [[Shayla]] - elvish+common hybrid
- jalin - ???

they NEVER look for a fight, even if the party starts it they spend all their actions to block/dodge and attempt reasoning with their attacker. use flavor to describe how missed hits are destroying their food, supplies, and tents.

#### Peaceful Resolutions
if the party decides to talk it out at the beginning, they gain new allies with the small band of orcs (mark on character sheet). [[Jukha]] explains the following:

	Our ancestor's transgressions are not indicative of their own free will, and  even their ancestors were misunderstood. Wave Echo Cave is a holy site to orckind, and an ancestral burial ground of the orc population on the Sword Coast. Many orcs make a pilgrimage to it at some point in their lives. However, when the humans, gnomes, and dwarves stumbled upon it, the strong, latent magical power was all they understood. As they began to harness it for the production of magical tools and weapons, our ancestors saw it as tampering with our theology and ancestry. Conversation on how to remedy this issue caused a divide amongst the orcs, with an aggressive militaristic force and an anti-violent community. However, it is the militaristic side that is remembered in your history, I'm sure. 

	We only seek now a place to call home. We caught wind of Cragmaw Castle and how it is hidden from your kinds, and thought it to be where our lineages may continue. However, the goblin infestation combined with the Black Spider's cunning defense is not something we can handle. We have no weapons, out of fear that holding any may be cause for action by adventurers such as yourselves. We seek only a place to build our community, free from the persecution of Phandalinites. 

	We have little to offer, but our scouting reports of Cragmaw Castle and the defenses they have. However, this information will not come free. We have no need for coin, only a place to nuture new life. If we allow this information to you, I ask on behalf of my people that you allow us to inhabit the new real estate. Should we be able to get back on our feet and have less hardship to tether our people, I could rally many of our former warriors to aid in your future causes. What say you, adventurers of intellect and reason?

#### Mid Battle Conversation
if the party talks it out mid battle, the orcs will ask for repayment of personal damages in the form of a quest. they desire food, and lots since orcs eat by barrel-load. queue hunting sidequest, listed below. after completion, they will point the direction of cragmaw castle, and invite the party to return to their camp in peace next time.

#### Hunting Sidequest
Either give one week of rations (7) per PC to the orcs, or go hunting for wild fowl, climaxing in an owlbear fight. the dead carcass should be brought back to the orc camp, after which [[Jukha]] will thank the adventurers for righting their wrongs, and invite them to return under peace next time. The quest is delayed until they NEED to come back post-Black Spider encounter at Wave Echo Cave to wrap up the moral dilemma presented at the end.
