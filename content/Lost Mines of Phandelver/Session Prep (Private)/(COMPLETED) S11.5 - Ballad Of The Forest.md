### Basics of Fry's Origin
- [[Moonshae Mercenary Corporation]] veteran
- druid circle south of the sword mountains, intensely focused on magical energies and the forging of them
- needs to bring one of the [[Three Great Relics]] to the [[Moonshae Isles]] to bring peace to the world (appease a greater god: [[Bahamut]]). specifically, needs the Blade.


### Intro to the Session
- odd bits and ends merchant at the stall is selling items that are intricately imbued with magical power and exude masterful craftsmanship; upon further inspection, fry is able to realize that these are former creations of his. 
	- questioning the druid leads to mostly just confusion, but he says he purchased these "useless trinkets" from a druid who hails from a circle in the [[Kryptgarden Forest]].
- States that the druid in question currently resides in Thundertree, yet said she would be around for the Midsummer's Night Eve Festival in Phandalin.
	- basic perception, investigation, insight, and charisma rolls find their way to the open air market of the [[Lionshield Coster of Phandalin]]. searching hard enough finds the stall of a druid who is running a repair stall/mechanical toy shop. 
		- Fry recognizes this druid, yet only in a way that they look related to someone they used to know well (this druid is the son of Fry's craftsmanship master)
- the druid is defensive and gives up no information, unless she hears about the roaring fire or is shown Fry's craftsmanship abilities.
	- either way, once the druid is less defensive/more curious, they will tell stories about the [[Ruined Circle of Kryptgarden]]. [[Fry]] will HOPEFULLY become more and more curious and suspicious, as the entire circle was wiped out 100 years ago and no one survived but him. 
	- It comes to a climax when the druid mentions one of the [[Three Great Relics]] by the name **"Blinded Blade"**. [[Fry]] quickly realizes that only his master knew that nickname for the blade he forged.
	- [[Fry]] hears a whisper, stating the following: **"I see now; thine experiences include the burning of the Kryptgarden. No matter: thou hast trespassed upon knowledge mine. I imagined thou growing learn'ed from the fires that thine master's curiosity smolder in. Now, lie witness to thine punishment for this treason, ye smithy of old. "**
	- Time seems to grow dilated and candles begin to dim. [[Fry]] is the only person who can move, hear, see, or process anything. Everyone else is effectively on pause.
		- Except [[MGs]], due to having resistance to time dilation.
	- The candles snuff out, and the shine of a dagger can be seen before the sounds of someone's slow, dying exhale. [[MGs]] and [[Fry]] both make DC10 Perception checks to see the figure or dagger.
		- Success: The flash of a coiled dagger that seems more like a drill than a blade, before the figure disappears in the flash of a red cloak that forms a pointed hood.
		- Failure: Just the blur of a cloaked figure swooping away from the body, and the brief shine of what looked like a spiraled blade
	- Time returns to normal and the candles are relit. The druid lies dead on the floor. The gnome clerk Poppy rushes to the doors of this open market, closing it off and announcing to all: 
		- **"MURDER!! BLOODY MURDER!! No one leaves the scene of the crime! The Fellows are here, and I'm sure they will figure everything out! But NOONE LEAVES UNTIL THE CULPRIT IS FOUND!!!"**
