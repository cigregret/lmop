### The Factions
1. [[Lord's Alliance]]
2. [[Zhentarim]]/Black Network
3. [[Mute Poets]]
4. [[Flaming Fists]]
5. [[Undermoor]]
6. [[Moonbahts]]

#### Lord's Alliance
Regional Leader: [[Open Lord Garakh Silverhand]]
Key NPCs: [[Sildar Hallwinter]]

Allies: [[Flaming Fists]], [[Mute Poets]] (sometimes)
Enemies: [[Zhentarim]], [[Moonbahts]]

Strengths:
- Can operate in the light and dark
- VERY rich, near infinite resources available at a moments notice
- Are the current political rulers of [[Waterdeep]], and have mostly positive public relationships with surrounding political powerhouses

Weaknesses
- morally ambiguous in how they reach their goals
	- uphold people like [[Gundren Rockseeker]] since it benefits their pockets
- secretive about goals even with long-term members ([[Shayla]], [[MGs]])
- are required to cover up everybody's messes and are the face of anything that happens

#### Zhentarim
Regional Leader: ???
Key NPCs: 

Allies: [[Undermoor]]
Enemies: [[Lord's Alliance]], [[Flaming Fists]]

Strengths:
- very quiet and unknown, small and tightly knit
- will work with anyone, but only until their needs wants are met
- have many illegal resources and rare items that others simply cannot get

Weaknesses
- persecuted endlessly for trafficking and purchase/sale/distribution of illegal items
- known for backstabbing
- not based in [[Waterdeep]], therefore have little available resources at a moment's notice

#### Mute Poets
Regional Leader: [[Smoke]]
Key NPCs: [[Jukha]]

Allies: [[Lord's Alliance]] (sometimes), [[Flaming Fists]] (shared enemies)
Enemies: [[Undermoor]], [[Moonbahts]]

Strengths
- have the backing of the common people (working class, unions, etc)
- decently capable to fight problems through economic means
- a section are magically adept and can provide strong assistance

Weaknesses
- not taken seriously by larger factions since they are fully local to [[Waterdeep]] besides personal relationships otherwise
- heavily lacking in resource fields such as military power since the [[Undermoor]] seceded from the organization
- are strongly challenged in political endeavors by stronger factions that wish to silence them (ie [[Lord's Alliance]])

#### Flaming Fists
Regional Leader: 
Key NPCs: 

Allies: [[Lord's Alliance]]
Enemies: [[Moonbahts]], [[Undermoor]]

Strengths
- across the board, the faction with the average strongest fighters (raw and magical)
- political strength from [[Baldur's Gate]], as well as near infinite resources available on a somewhat short notice
- have many allies, and no one wants to be the enemy of them

Weaknesses
- very centrist and lacking in any definitive stance
- are really just there to report to [[Baldur's Gate]] and give non-partisan assistance to their allies, and are strongly limited by their direction
- are not very personable since they are trained soldiers first

#### Undermoor
Regional Leader: [[Kite]]
Key NPCs: 

Allies: [[Zhentarim]]
Enemies: [[Mute Poets]]

Strengths
- second strongest in terms of raw fighting power
- know many secrets to other factions due to being a collective of spies
	- formed from the defective members of other factions, mainly those who thought that the [[Mute Poets]] were too soft for wanting to enact change non-violently
- rumored to have the Glass in their possession

Weaknesses
- no magically adept individuals besides their leader
- high-priority target for everyone since they are rumored to have the Glass
- does not have many resources besides manpower and assorted gangs

Regional Leader: 
Key NPCs: 

Allies: n/a
