One: I do not trust your mortal hands to prepare a suitable dish. Therefore, you will have a number of rooms to traverse in order to learn my recipe of choice. ### Cooking Sequence

#### Room 1
In the center of this room are four sculpted pedestals. Each resembled a humanoid figure kneeling down, presenting a tray towards the large statue with two doors on either side. The pedestals are engraved as follows:
- A figure with a veiled face. It’s hands seem to be poked and scarred. By it’s feet rests a small censor which gushes smoke.
- A figure that appears naked, save for some shorts. It appears to sink slightly into the floor. By it’s feet rests a net and a spear.
- A figure that, while kneeling seems braced on a cane. At its feet rests a large clay pot.
- A figure wearing a wide hat, a sickle and a wooden flail rests at its feet.

When the players placed an item on the tray, if it was correct there was a very slight sound of shifting stone. If it was wrong, nothing happened. If they tried to move past the statue, it would attack.

The players clued in pretty quickly that they needed to find the proper 'tribute' from the alcove on the wall and place it on the proffered trays, corresponding to the pedestal. The answers for each, and other thoughts on how they played out are listed below.

- The first pedestal's answer was a honeycomb, as it was a beekeeper. They really thought was a priest for a long time, and this may be because 'censor' was the wrong word. This was the last one they got, and they failed checks to get the hint that the hands were scarred by stings. The hint that finally got them was, once the other three were known, insinuating that all the answers had something in common (food).
- The second pedestal's answer was fish. They got that nearly instantly, but the hint I had for them was that the pedestal 'sinking into the floor' insinuated the figure might not be on dry land.
- The third pedestal's answer was cows, though I ended up accepting goats. With the cane and the pot, they really thought it was a beggar, and they really only got it once I gave them the hint that the cane was rather long and curved (think a Shepard's crock), with the pot being for milk. Really, any milk producing herd animal is likely correct.
- The fourth pedestal's answer was wheat/grain. They also got this quickly, though if they were unsure describing the flail appearing less like a weapon than a tool might help.

Upon completion, the large statue in the center presents spices and seeds as listed below:
- Salt and pepper
- mustard seed
- olive oil
- mushrooms

#### Room 2
A smoky room with a large censer that spews smoke, making it hard to see. The room has an identical statue to the far end, with two more doors on either side. There are four buckets at the foot of the statue, with various liquids in each of them.
- water
	- solves the puzzle
- oil
	- causes the golem's effective range to become doubled, as well as doing 2d10 fire damage on a failed save, and half that on a successful one.
- milk
	- causes a stench of spoiled milk to fill the room, poisoning all targets for one minute
- honey
	- causes any targets to be slowed, reducing movement speed to 5ft for the next minute.

Roll Initative. Approaching the censer causes a gust to blow them back, and the censer forms into a golem. The golem simply attempts to push anyone who gets within a 10 foot range back away from it and towards the door with a DC 15 Strength check for every 5 feet closer to the golem. The golem takes no damage, and the solution is to pour water onto the censer to put the fire out, causing the golem to fall apart and the core to remain behind. Placing the core on the statue's tray solves the puzzle, and the golem presents eggs and puff pastry.
