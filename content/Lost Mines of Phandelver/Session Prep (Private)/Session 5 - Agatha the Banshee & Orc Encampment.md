#### Archibald
	You see visions of battle. Not any war that you have heard of, or a fight you have been part of, but see indiscernable figures cutting each other down. You feel the valor, the adrenaline, the cravings of dominance. This carnal desire is to seek glory in the subjugation of your enemies. You have felt it before, even stronger every time you have cast your divine smite. Yet now, it is intoxicating and you are lulled into the pleasures of war. you hear a voice:
	"Douse the flame of hope. Tis ne'er enough to merely defeat thine enemy in battle; thine victory must be so overwhelming, thine foes' will to fight is shattered forever. A blade can end a life, but fear can topple an empire."
	You feel a searing pain on your bicep.
	"Rule with an iron fist. Once thy hast conquered, tolerate no dissent. Thine word is law. Those whom obey it shall be favored. Those who defy it shall be punished, as an example to all whom might course."
	The searing pain gets hotter, as if an open flame lays upon it.
	"Strength above all. Thou shall rule whislt no stronger arises. If a stronger is arisen, thy might not but grow mightier to meet thine challenge, or fall to thy own wrack."
	It feels as if your arm is being branded, as the flames lick your skin.
	"Long and hard didst thou fight. Now off, tyrant of knights, iron monger of dragon. Aye, thy grim oath hath been sworn of conquest. Now off, 'i mine name as ye Angel of the Seven Heavens, ye Justicemaker; Bahamut, ye God of All Dragons."
	You awaken and check your right bicep that felt such intense flame. You see the three tenets of your oath seared into your arm, as if branded by the fire of a dragon.

#### Madame Wonder
	As you drift to sleep, you reminisce the nothic you encountered just hours prior. The nothic's probing into your mind, your past, your secrets, your murders, your loss, all of it overwhelms you in your sleep. That devilish Infernal seeps into your mind. The whispers grow louder.
	In the battle with Mosk the bugbear, you reached closer to death than you ever had before, seemingly only needing to reach our her hand to embrace it. The whispers grow louder.
	 Incapacitated now not by sleep but by the overwhelming pressure of the nothic, the experience of touching Lady Death's fingertips, the cacophony of unbridled screams ringing in your mind, you begin to give up all hope of continuing. Then, a voice resonates in her mind. Yet this voice was not the harsh Infernal speak of a fiend, but was speaking in a gentle, soothing Common. 
	"My shadowborne, you have endured enough. Even existing on the Material Plane, free of fey light and the mirrors of dark, should not mean you are forsaken of any solace.
	Take my hand, my shadowborne. You have birthed yourself anew as Madame Wonder, yet the Nine Hells have refused to allot that respect. Take my hand and be free of the Infernal hatred, live as you were born to. Take my hand, my gladiator."
	You reach for the outstretched slender fingers that have no wrist nor arm. As you grasped the fingertips of this voice, the Infernal screams dissipate instantly, replaced by something you have never heard: silence. Then the voice speaks again:
	"I will call on you when you are most needed, and will not sully your pain with meaningless tasks. Now rise, my shadowborne. Enter battle for the Raven Queen, bearing the title of her esteemed gladiator."
	As you awaken, you find an unfamiliar weapon in your hand, yet you're holding it tightly. The voice speaks once more, as if naming the weapon:
	"The Blade of Chained Shadows"

#### MGs
	You wake up, feeling well rested after your adventure in the Feywilde. Gazing out of your window at the moonlight, you feel a chill escape through the cracks of the sill. This is nothing that bothers your young dwarven body. However, your eyes, keen even in the dark, spot a line of small glowing particles slipping through the window's crack. These particles seem strangely attracted to you, following your hand as you move. You hold still, watching as they slowly form an ethereal bracelet, swirling around your wrist. You feel... protected. Looking outside, you bear witness to a large cylinder of them pointing towards a small smattering of trees about fifty feet away, glowing underneath the moon's gaze.

Do you investigate? 

	As you open the window, the particles begin to surround both of your arms entirely, forming a sort of armor around you. Jumping out the window with a reckless abandon, you are caught by more of these particles that now stick to your feet, reoganizing themselves to best suit your current needs. As you jog to the origin of these particles, you collect more and more of these particles around your body, and by the time you arrive at this grove your entire body is covered in a thin glowing veil.

Do you search for the origin of the particles?

	As you look down at the bush that these glowing dots are coming from, you smell something. Something... rotting. The smell of rotting flesh. As you continue to dig through the underbrush, you notice plants reacting to your druidic influence and part ways for you, revealing... Iarno's body. His head is gone, as it was when Sildar killed him, but his neck down has been dumped here. That's when you notice the particles start to swim in the air, hovering around fungi that have sprouted as he decomposes. These particles seem to be the spores of such a fungi- but why are they surrounding you?
	Almost as you ask that question, Iarno's body twitches. The glowing spores surrounding your body briefly lash out, as if tying your armor to Iarno's body. Then, it twiches again. And again. Until, slowly but surely, it stands. Before you stands a the headless body of a man that you briefly saw. But now it stands before you, as if its... waiting.
