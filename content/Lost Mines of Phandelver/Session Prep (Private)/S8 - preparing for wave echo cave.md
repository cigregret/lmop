### Mimic Museum
Museum that is filled to the brim with mimics. All sorts of trinkets, showcases, and sculptures are mimics. important thing to remember is that mimics exude a strong, yet hardly visually noticeable adhesive that forces a strength check

begin with fragile mimics that are small items. have the damage be reasonable. use a basic 1d6+2 of appropriate typing for physical blunt attacks, such as a bench ramming into a pc

slowly work the way up, with larger exhibits and sculptures making their way into the fray. eventually, the larger ones have tentacles that protrude from them with a 10ft melee range, and force an adhesive grapple on attack (DC13). 

Finally, the BBEG is 2 different walls, with a shared hp pool, that slowly approach them from either side of a closed room. it can be quickly defeated by looking up at the ceiling and attacking the "brain", a thin cord that drips adhesive from the ceiling and connects the two walls. any attacks made against this cord are double damage, and it is weak to slashing.
