#### intro to grok and will
find their way to alderleaf farms, owned by an elderly halfling named Mister Alderleaf. he is an apprehensive, bigoted man who is visibly uncomfortable with the diversity of the party, specifically madame wonder. slip in some microaggressions, such as referring to wonder as "hellspawn" and largely ignoring them if they talk. 
	if he is pressed about these microaggressions, he gets extremely defensive and tells the party to get off his property if they arent interested in helping. however, he will warn that if they dont help an "innocent old man" whose family has been here since the Phandelver's Pact, then the whole town will know and get involved.
	If the party continues to press him, he yells out for the local guards and wants them trespassed. enter @grok, who comes out to *somewhat* defuse the situation and calm mr alderleaf down.
whether the fellas are pressed about the microaggressions or not, it should lead to @grok "leading" the party to the cows and supplying them with the information they need. after some investigation, they can find either 
1. arcane residue in the form of wisps that curl off the cows and point off the property
2. a physical burrow under the fence that the cows could fit through, along with tracks that lead further away

either way, they should find themselves nearby the graveyard. a large weeping willow tree hangs over this side of the graveyard, and the tracks/wisps lead to its base. 
As they get closer
1. the light of day start to dim
2. the wisps, whether seen or unseen up until this point, are visible to all and congeal into a door
These effects are reversed if an individual chooses to step back. if anyone touches the door, a deadly cold envelopes around the whole party, casting a blanket of magical darkness in a 15 foot radius around them. they then feel their bodies begin to shift, yet not in a way that is entirely physical. 
	MGs and Madame Wonder are to roll history checks (DC 7) to see if they recognize it. on success, they know it as the same feeling as when they entered the feywilde: they are being planeshifted against their will.
Also, there is a surprise visit from the Raven Queen! This time, everyone sees the spectral hand reach out, undoubtedly reaching towards madame wonder. then, she speaks to no one in particular, but with her skeletal fingers mere inches away from madame wonder's face
	"Do not accept any propositions with the heretic you are about to meet. He seeks nothing more than a satisfactory meal for him and his troupe. This posse of gourmets reside within my domain, yet refuse to acknowledge the contract of the land. Deal with them as you like, but should any one of you become theirs I will not save you. I give none of you assistance further; my gladiator is proving enough for my amusement. Accepting clauses from these animals will make you enemy of mine and hers."

	As the smog begins to clear, you see a grand manor not even thirty paces in front of you. Large balconies adorn the second story, while ivory pillars contrast against the ash wood of the building itself. You see bushes, carved into shapes of various animals and even monsters, some of which look entirely unfamiliar to you all. As you try to focus your eyes on these bushes, you notice small patches of odd-looking flowers amongst the greenery. Feeling like your vision seems to still be hazy, you maybe blink or rub your eyes; however, you notice that without a doubt, the flowers are gray. As you resurvey your surroundings, you realize that the ash wood and ivory you saw earlier seems almost too muted, and the air begins to weigh heavy and cold upon your neck and shoulders. 
	Looking around for any other indicators as to where you are, or what is happening to you, a voice speaks.
	"Well hello my little cuties... Welcome to the Manoir of Gourmets. My name is Monseiur Mordre. I am the leader, if you will, of Le Detachement de Gourmets."

Monseiur Mordre is a human male, reaching about six feet and change. Slender and lean, he has somewhat pale skin, yet you cannot tell how pale due to the effects clouding your vision
