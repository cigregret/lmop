A dwarven company specializing in construction and mass production of specialty goods. More specifically, the [[Rockseeker Brothers]] have a great interest in the magic tools and weapons industry. The company is known for their skilled renovations of abandoned/destroyed structures, even being able to preserve artifacts and ruins for archeological or museum purposes. They are estimated to have built 17% of the residential and commercial buildings in [[Baldur's Gate]], 11% of all buildings in [[Waterdeep]], and 25% of the residential sector in [[Neverwinter]]. They frequently take on specialty/custom jobs, most recently looking into renovating [[Phandalin]] for the expected job openings that reopening the [[Forge of Spells]] will create.


Their recent business exploits have resulted in the current head of operations, [[Gundren Rockseeker]], supposedly locating the fabled [[Wave Echo Cave]] and [[Forge of Spells]] within. He was reportedly kidnapped on the road to [[Phandalin]] under the [[Black Spider]]'s orders, and taken by the [[Cragmaw Tribe]] to [[Cragmaw Castle]]. The other two brothers, [[Tharden Rockseeker]] and [[Nundro Rockseeker]], were supposed to arrive in [[Phandalin]] before him but have also gone missing. They were last seen being harassed by [[Redbrands]].
