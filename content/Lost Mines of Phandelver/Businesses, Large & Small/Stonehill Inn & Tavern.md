The **Stonehill Inn & Tavern** is a two-story building located in [[Phandalin]]. The first floor, which has no doors and is open to the [[Phandalin]] town square, is the tavern. The second floor, accessible only by a single staircase next to the tavern's bar, is a small inn with eight rooms.

#### Tavern
The tavern section is run by a [[Brass Dragonborn|brass dragonborn]] named [[Gunther]]. As loud as he is, he is extremely passionate about his bar and mysteriously is able to imports exclusive ales and meads, including [[Dwarves' Blood]]. He also serves basic meals for customers of the inn side of Stonehill.

#### Inn
The inn side is run by a middle-aged [[Human|human]] named [[Toblen Stonehill]]. He takes care of renting out the rooms, and being the owner of the entire establishment, pays [[Gunther]] weekly. The inn is the only one in [[Phandalin]], yet sees little use as the rough-and-tumble frontier town has nothing a tourist would desire, nor serves as a rest stop for weary travelers being that [[Phandalin]] sits at the end of an offshoot of the [[Triboar Trail]].
