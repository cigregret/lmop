A legendary forge of a past age, said to reside within [[Wave Echo Cave]]. Historical accounts state that the forge was used to create powerful magic items through [[Phandelver's Pact]] between the humans, gnomes, and dwarves of [[Phandalin]]. Nowadays, the location of [[Wave Echo Cave]] is lost, therefore the forge is as well. There are rumors of those who know the location, notably [[Gundren Rockseeker]] of [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] and the infamous [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]].
