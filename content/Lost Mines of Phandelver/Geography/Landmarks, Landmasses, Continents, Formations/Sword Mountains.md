A mountain range found between the cities of [[Neverwinter]] and [[Waterdeep]], near the central [[Sword Coast]]. The town of [[Phandalin]] sits near the mountains' base, and the highest peak of the **Sword Mountains** is known as [[Icespire Peak]]. A powerfully arcane dragon of lore is said to reside there, leaving much of the **Sword Mountains** uninhabited out of fear.
