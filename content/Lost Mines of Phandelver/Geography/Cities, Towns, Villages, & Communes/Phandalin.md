### Basic History
**Phandalin** is an old mining town located in the [[Material Plane]], around one week of consistent carriage travel from [[Neverwinter]]. Formerly, this half-abandoned town was a bustling metropolis thanks to [[Phandelver's Pact]], yet that was several centuries ago. After the orcs attacked the [[Forge of Spells]], numerous cave-ins caused the location of [[Wave Echo Cave]] to be lost to time and the region to grow deserted. 

Many treasure hunters have attempted to locate [[Wave Echo Cave]] in the centuries since its location was lost, yet seemingly to no avail. **Phandalin** was resettled in the past few decades, becoming a rough-and-tumble frontier town. More importantly, however, was the new presence of a dwarven trio: the [[Rockseeker Brothers]]. Led by [[Gundren Rockseeker]], they claim to have discovered the location of [[Wave Echo Cave]]. With the help of the [[Lord's Alliance]], they intend on reopening the [[Forge of Spells]] to bring economic prosperity back to the region. This effort is known as the [[Rockseeker Initiative]]. 

The **Phandalin** of today is largely self-sufficient, having enough harvest from its various farms to support its small population. The [[Tresendar Family]] that protected the town is no more, leaving the [[Phandalin Townmaster's Hall]] to post bounties in hope of protection from the nearby orc populations. As of late, a human gang known as the [[Redbrands]] found their way to **Phandalin** and began harassing the population and businesses. The current [[Townmaster]], [[Harbin Wester]], has remained politically impartial to the matter. However, a group of adventurers commissioned by [[Sildar Hallwinter]] of the [[Lord's Alliance]] recently found the [[Redbrands]]' hideout and brought their leader, a wizard known as [[Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek|Glasstaff]], to a swift end.
 
### Town Geography
![[Phandalin Map.png]]
**Phandalin** sits along an offshoot of where the [[Triboar Trail]] grows close to the [[Sword Mountains]]. It sits at the base of these mountains, within the foothills that [[Wave Echo Cave]] is rumored to  inhabit. To the southwest is the [[Mere of Dead Men]], and to the north grows the [[Neverwinter Wood]]. Taking the [[Triboar Trail]] west will eventually put a traveler onto [[The High Road]], and north from there is the city of [[Neverwinter]].

Most buildings in **Phandalin** are in use, but spotted ruins remind residents and visitors that they live within relics from a past age. The modern day town exists within an 800ft$^2$ footprint, with [[Tresendar Manor]] sitting upon a hill to the east, somewhat separated to the rest of the town. There are several functional businesses, most notably including [[Denrar Smithy & Jeweler]], [[Stonehill Inn & Tavern]], [[Barthen's Provisions]], the [[Lionshield Coster of Phandalin]], [[Phandalin Miner's Exchange]], [[Alderleaf Farm & Orchard]], and the newly established branch of [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] Aside from businesses, there are some other landmarks in **Phandalin**, including a [[Shrine of Luck]], the [[Phandalin Townmaster's Hall]], [[Sleeping Giant Tavern]], and [[Tresendar Manor]]. Otherwise, the rest of the buildings in town serve as homes for those who live and work at the various businesses, or run the farms on the outskirts of town. 


### Population & Culture
The population of **Phandalin** is notably evenly split amongst gnomes, dwarves, and humans. They live in relative harmony with one another, and hold a shared culture unlike larger cities such as [[Neverwinter]], [[Waterdeep]], or [[Baldur's Gate]], where races and species firmly hold on to the culture from their homelands. Instead, the culture of **Phandalin** is built strongly on the mining history of the area, and the appreciation of all roles in their society that allows for their unique self-sufficiency.
