An expansive city located north of [[Baldur's Gate]] and south of [[Neverwinter]] on the [[Sword Coast]]. On initial perspective, it is not as large as [[Baldur's Gate]], but has the largest port on the [[Sword Coast]] as well as expands underneath the nearby mountain. Including the many layers of city within the mountain it is estimated that [[Waterdeep]] could rival [[Baldur's Gate]] in size, yet still falls quite short in terms of population.

Recently has been politically tumultuous due to the [[Open Lord of Waterdeep]], [[Open Lord Garakh Silverhand|Garakh Silverhand]], also being the founder of the [[Lord's Alliance]]. Additionally, many citizens of [[Waterdeep]] and surrounding areas criticize her liberal usage of non-proportional military and economic power when putting a stop to any budding problems.
