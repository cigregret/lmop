The castle where [[King Grol]] resides, the capital of the [[Cragmaw Tribe]]. All orders come from within the castle, save those that come from wherever the [[Black Spider]] may be *to* [[Cragmaw Castle]]. Very few creatures, even those within the [[Cragmaw Tribe]], know where [[Cragmaw Castle]] is located.
