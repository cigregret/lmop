An indecipherable map imbued with great Fey magic, said to lead its holder to [[Three Great Relics#The Book|The Book]]. However, most anyone who has looked at this map is quickly filled with nausea, then an overwhelming exhaustion that quickly turns to a magical coma. The map itself is described as picturing a dense web of scribbles, with a centered dot that slowly inches along the lines.

It is said to be currently held by [[MGs]].
