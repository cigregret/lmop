### Overview
A quintet of gourmets who had their own [[Domains of Dread|Domain of Dread]], and a collective pact with the [[Raven Queen]]. They seemingly met their end by her hand, however, after failing a cooking challenge against the [[Phandalin Fellows]] that they had suggested (see [[Session 10 - Cooking With Shar]]). 

However, their leader [[Le Detachement De Gourmets#Members|Monsieur Mordre]] was the summoner of the now infamous [[Anti-Bald]], yet it is unknown as to how. 


### Members
- Monsieur Mordre (Chief Executive Officer)
- Monseiur Jean (Chief of Public Relations & Communication)
- Monseiur Louis (Chief of Human Relations)
- Monseiur Gabriel (Chief of Treasury)
- Monseiur Antione (Chief of Research)
