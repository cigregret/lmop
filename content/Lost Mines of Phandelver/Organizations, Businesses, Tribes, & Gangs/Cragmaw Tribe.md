The tribe of goblins, ruled by [[King Grol]], that reside in the area from the [[Neverwinter Wood]] to the [[Sword Mountains]]. Strong ties to the [[Redbrands]] and the [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]]. The location of their royal castle, [[Cragmaw Castle]] is not widely known besides existing somewhere in the [[Neverwinter Wood]].
