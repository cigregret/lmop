There are many unknown lords and minor deities that are imprisoned within demiplanes inside the [[Shadowfell]]; these are known as **Domain of Dread**. Examples of them, besides the [[Fugue]], cannot be given as those who are able to enter and exit freely are typically in control of these undead paradises and keep their secrets closer than any other creature. It is assumed that [[The Dark Powers]] are the wardens and creators of all **Domains of Dread**, but the imprisoned evil is still typically at full power within their **Domain of Dread**
