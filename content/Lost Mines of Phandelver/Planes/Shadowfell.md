#### Overview
A mirror of the [[Material Plane]], in contrast with the [[Feywilde]]. It is the transitory and final plane for mortal souls as they make their way to the [[City of Judgement]] in the [[Fugue]] demiplane. Therefore, the inhabitants of **Shadowfell** are rarely living, and those creatures who are animated are typically souls that escaped judgement and clung onto what little life force they had remaining. Very rarely do creatures exist that are  created as natural life. 

Within the **Shadowfell**, there are many god-like beings and even actual gods. Notable ones are listed below, with their main role in parentheses:
- [[Shar]] (Creator of the **Shadowfell**, Goddess of Darkness)
- [[Raven Queen]] (Current Host of Shadow, Goddess of Death & Afterlife)
- [[Vecna]] (Goddess of Secrets & Undead)
- [[The Dark Powers]] (Wardens of Powerful Evil, Creators of the [[Domains of Dread]])

#### Geography
Not much is yet documented, but it can be assumed that due to the **Shadowfell** being a dark mirror of the [[Material Plane]], it would bear contrasting resemblance to the same locations within the [[Material Plane]]. 

Light is naught in this realm: bringing or creating light sources into the **Shadowfell** will halve their standard brightness, and quickly attracts undead who wish to savor the light of life again. However, traveling without light can lead to living mortals slowly losing their sanity as the souls of the dead roam freely around their ears and play with their senses.

#### Population
The inhabitants of **Shadowfell** are rarely living, and those creatures who are animated are typically souls that escaped judgement and clung onto what little life force they had remaining. Very rarely do creatures exist that are created as natural life, and they are typically relocated from other planes as pets for gods or entertainment for those imprisoned within [[Domains of Dread]]. Exceptions can be made, as always, but are even rarer than entering the plane itself.
