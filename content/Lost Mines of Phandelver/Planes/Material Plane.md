**The Material Plane** is the main plane that holds most humanoid races and species. It is considered, by the many who live upon it and by the universe's laws, the core plane. The [[Shadowfell]] and the [[Feywilde]] are mirrors of this plane. Thus, a particular location would be contrasted or exaggerated in various ways when seen in the [[Shadowfell]] or [[Feywilde]] as compared to the **Material Plane**. Time is considered the most constant when measuring the ages of creatures and time on the **Material Plane**, and the least variability and convolution exists within the natural order.
