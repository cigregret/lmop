#### History
The Archangel of the Seven Heavens, the Justicemaker, the God of All Dragons. Bahamut is the pinnacle of justice and honor, and oversees the entire dragon population from [[Mount Celestia]]. He is the venerated god of [[Mount Celestia]], and the entire plane exists due to his sheer will to create a place of law and order.

As the tale goes, he was banished long ago for ruling the [[Material Plane]] with an iron fist, dousing all hope of resistance, and embodying the tenet of strength above all. A conflict between the [[Mirrored Planes]] led to his banishment, and split the [[Mirrored Planes]] into their current state.
