#### Overview
An Archfey of the Court who at one point transported [[MGs]] and [[Madame Wonder]] to the Feywilde to participate in his [[Arena of Wild Magic]]. After they defeated Quicklings, Redcaps, and a Faerie Dragon, he gave the two his name to call upon should they wish to be instantly swept back to the [[Arena of Wild Magic]] and battle again for rewards.

Known as the Prince of Fools, Cerise is uncharacteristically aligned with the [[Material Plane]] senses of morality. However, his fey ancestry and background shine when he decides to interfere with world calamities for the "sake" of the [[Material Plane]]. He is the commissioner and owner of the [[Three Great Relics]], yet it is unknown why he had them made in the first place. He also is said to be the [[Feywilde|Feywilde's]] resident expert on the [[Feyfell]], and he holds a special interest in the [[Insects of Feyfell]] yet supposedly knows little more than anyone else in the field.
