#### Basic Information
A rumored elven smithy supposedly located within the [[Kryptgarden Forest]], and guarded by the druidic circle there. The rumors state that this immortal being could craft magical items of any kind using only the smallest bits of metal, and the arcane energy of the nature around them. It is also rumored that they frequented the [[Feywilde]], hence their extended lifetime.

#### Confirmed Details
This smithy was commissioned by [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools]] to create one of the [[Three Great Relics]]: [[Three Great Relics#The Sword of Blind Fog (The Blade)|The Sword of Blind Fog (The Blade)]]. [[Fry]] was also one of this smithy's apprentices, before the [[Kryptgarden Forest]] was mostly burned down by [[Heretic Cultists of Bahamut]].
