An immortal banshee that occasionally resides in a lone cottage along the [[Triboar Trail]]. A mystic with the ability to truthfully answer any question, yet does not do so without reason. Cost is naught to her; an answer from **Agatha** solely lies upon catching her in a good mood and having a gift to give. However, it is said that every few centuries she takes note of particular individuals, following them through journeys of myth as an observer and infrequent informant. To those parties, she is said to perform fortune tellings about the darkest moments to come in their futures.

The [[Phandalin Fellows]] were able to uncover more of her powers, as she seems to be someone with enough power to forge warlock contracts with mortals. One such mortal, the gnome librarian known as Blinker, was outed as a warlock. However, as soon as this came to be she was seemingly replaced by the familiar elvish spectre that Agatha chooses to represent herself with. After Agatha conferred further information about the [[Three Great Relics]] to the [[Phandalin Fellows]], she left and returned Blinker to her normal duties.
