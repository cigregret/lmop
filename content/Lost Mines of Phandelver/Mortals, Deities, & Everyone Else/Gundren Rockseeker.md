#### History
The co-founder and current head of operations at [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]], and one of the famous [[Rockseeker Brothers]] (along with his younger brothers [[Tharden Rockseeker|Tharden]] and [[Nundro Rockseeker|Nundro]]). His specialty within the business is his outgoing and social nature that stands in stark contrast of his master crafter brothers. This makes him the perfect candidate to lead the company, while his brothers focus on their strengths that allow the company to grow.

He claims to know the location of [[Wave Echo Cave]], and formed the [[Rockseeker Initiative]] with the [[Lord's Alliance]] to redevelop and reopen the [[Forge of Spells]] for economic prosperity. He originally set out to travel with [[Sildar Hallwinter]] to [[Phandalin]] to begin the search, but was kidnapped along the way by [[Cragmaw Tribe]] goblins on the [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]]'s orders. From there, he was transported to [[Cragmaw Castle]] where his fate is currently unknown. However, his person and his map of [[Wave Echo Cave]] were last located at [[Cragmaw Castle]]. 

[[Sildar Hallwinter]] was dispatched as a liaison for the [[Lord's Alliance]], but also to provide assistance to **Gundren** in proving the location of [[Wave Echo Cave]]. **Gundren** also employed three mercenaries (being [[Fry]], [[Archibald]], and [[Madame Wonder]]) through [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] to assist with this undertaking.

#### Current Standing
Through the sole acquisition of the [[Forge of Spells]], Gundren was able to grow an empire from his newfound monopoly of the production of magical items across the [[Sword Coast]]. He used this wealth and power to establish the sprawling metropolis of [[New Phandalin]], and take place amongst the [[Lord's Alliance#Organization|Lioncrowns]] of the [[Lord's Alliance]]. 

However, this relationship grew tired quickly, as his greed knows no bounds. He quickly looks to usurp the entire [[Lord's Alliance]] from its founder, [[Open Lord Garakh Silverhand]] of [[Waterdeep]]. This has led to an internal conflict within the [[Lord's Alliance]], birthing a battle of spy networks and information known as the [[Cold Lord's War]].
