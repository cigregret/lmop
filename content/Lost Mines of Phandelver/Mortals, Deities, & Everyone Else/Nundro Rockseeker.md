The youngest of the [[Rockseeker Brothers]], and the one most well attuned to the arcane. Head of magical goods at [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]], he talks a bit too much for his own good. Upon hearing of his eldest brother, [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]], supposedly finding the [[Forge of Spells]], he gushed about it nonstop to anyone who would listen, racing to reach [[Phandalin]] as soon as possible so he could begin experimenting with new possibilities in the [[Forge of Spells]]. 

Disappeared from his temporary residence in [[Phandalin]] along with his older brother [[Tharden Rockseeker|Tharden]] before [[Gundren Rockseeker]] and [[Sildar Hallwinter]] were kidnapped just outside of town. Was last seen in an argument with [[Redbrands]].
