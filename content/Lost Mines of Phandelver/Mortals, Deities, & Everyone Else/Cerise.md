A mysterious Fey who at one point transported [[MGs]] and [[Madame Wonder]] to the Feywilde to participate in his [[Arena of Wild Magic]]. After they defeated Quicklings, Redcaps, and a Faerie Dragon, he gave the two his name to call upon should they wish to be instantly swept back to the [[Arena of Wild Magic]] and battle again for rewards.
