A slightly bigoted, halfling barbarian known as a folk hero of [[Phandalin]], to which he is a native. Working as a farmhand underneath [[Mister Alderleaf]], he encountered the [[Phandalin Fellows]] during an odd job concerning some cows. He has little in the way of a hero's journey or adventurer's exploits to share, instead seemingly looking for a way out of [[Phandalin]]. Joining the [[Phandalin Fellows]], albeit by the command of [[Gundren Rockseeker]] and [[Sildar Hallwinter]], he now ventures into [[Wave Echo Cave]] with the [[Phandalin Fellows]] to reclaim it as a joint operation between [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] and the [[Lord's Alliance]].
