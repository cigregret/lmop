**Shayla** is a [[Half-Elf|half-elf]] with rouge-ish tendencies. Hailing from [[Waterdeep]], she is a [[Lord's Alliance#Organization|Crow]] of the [[Lord's Alliance]] and is rumored to be the adoptive daughter of [[Sildar Hallwinter]]. Little information is factually known about her, besides that **"Shayla"** is not her real name. 

She currently has been dispatched by the [[Lord's Alliance]] to [[Phandalin]] along with [[MGs]] and [[Sildar Hallwinter]]. The reported purpose of this mission is to assist [[Gundren Rockseeker]] of [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] in reopening the [[Forge of Spells]], presumably to bring economic prosperity to the central [[Sword Coast]]. 

In her interview to become a [[Lord's Alliance#Organization|Crow]], she notably said nothing at all. However, she was a unanimous decision to undergo training and become part of the [[Lord's Alliance]]. Some raised objections, but those criticisms were quickly silenced seemingly out of nowhere.

### Rumors
As previously mentioned, **Shayla** is rumored to be the adoptive daughter of [[Sildar Hallwinter]]. As she supposedly hails from [[Waterdeep]], same as [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]], it is reasonable to think they may have met before she joined the [[Lord's Alliance]]. However, [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] has not answered any questions on the matter, merely stating the following:

*"She is a Crow with valuable skills. Those skills should be given ample resources to be honed, not let to rust away in the slums."*

It is also said that her quick wit, swordsmanship skills, and general skullduggery are derived from a childhood at sea, as when she does talk her accent is notably [[Moonshae Isles|Moonshae]]. A series of rumors depicting her as a child stowaway taken into a pirate crew circulate the [[Sword Coast]], and would explain her lack of official records.

Nothing else is known about her at this time.
