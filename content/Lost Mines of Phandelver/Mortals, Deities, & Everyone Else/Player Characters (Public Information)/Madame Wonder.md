A [[Tiefling|tiefling]], and formerly a well-known gladiator of the [[Baldur's Gate Grand Arena]]. She was removed from the arena and barred from competition due her casting an eldritch blast on a human barbarian. It is unknown why she performed such an action, but to publicly showcase any powers given via warlock contract with a deity is clear reason to be barred from gladiatorial competitions. 

Not much is known about her besides her gladiator past. It has been confirmed that "**Madame Wonder**" is not her real name, but is her chosen title when she first started as a gladiator; however, her real name is not publicly known. She worked odd jobs around [[Baldur's Gate]] for coin, eventually ending up as a contractor for [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] This connection led to her being put on a team directly under [[Gundren Rockseeker]], with the purpose of delivering supplies for the [[Rockseeker Initiative]] from [[Baldur's Gate]] to [[Phandalin]]. The other two members of this three-person party are the human paladin [[Archibald]] and the dwarf cleric [[Fry]]. 

Throughout her time in [[Phandalin]], **Madame Wonder**, [[Archibald]], and [[Fry]] have managed to accomplish several great feats, including saving [[Sildar Hallwinter]] from a goblin den and putting an end to the [[Redbrands]] terrorizing [[Phandalin]]. She is known to be the flashy and performative member of the group, yet upon returning from the [[Redbrands]]' hideout she reportedly grew quieter and more solemn. This led to rumors, that were later confirmed, that she had escaped her pact with a Great Fiend by entering the [[Raven Queen]]'s service.

It is rumored that this change of pact has caused her to drag the other members of the [[Phandalin Fellows]] to the [[Shadowfell]]. These rumors carry great weight as the respected [[Mister Alderleaf]] initiated their creation after he requested the [[Phandalin Fellows]]' help with some cows on his farm.
