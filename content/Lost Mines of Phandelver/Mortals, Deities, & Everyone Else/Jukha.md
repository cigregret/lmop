#### History
An orc whose ancestral line has always been characteristically non-nomadic, which is a significant subversion of typical orc culture. However, his line was tasked long ago by an orc  demigod to protect [[Wave Echo Cave]], the ancient burial ground of their people and theorized birthplace of all orckind. As their numbers grew thin across the centuries due to persecution and genocide, the dozen or so orc left hid within the [[Neverwinter Wood]] to survive. Meeting [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]] and [[Gunther]] caused him to join the [[Zhentarim]] in hopes of changing the hearts of the people of [[Phandalin]] to allow his people to live near their ancestral homeland and graveyard. 

Not long after, he met the [[Phandalin Fellows]] and enlisted their help in negotiations with [[Gundren Rockseeker]] in an attempt to solve the conflict diplomatically. However, after the party disappeared in [[Wave Echo Cave]], he was left with only [[Doormat]], [[Droop]], and [[Gunther]]. As [[Gundren Rockseeker]] proclaimed himself the "Venerated Lord of New Phandalin", he teamed up with his remaining allies and formed the [[Fellas of the Fellows]] with the sole purpose of denying [[Gundren Rockseeker]] his goals.
