#### History
A goblin kidnapped by [[Archibald]] after an attempted ambush of them on the [[Triboar Trail]]. Later was given severe blunt force trauma and taken to [[Phandalin]] with the initial trio, trained in the art of [[Dragonchess]], and made [[Archibald]]'s squire. It is during the introduction of this chessboxing ceremony that the goblin was named **Doormat**.

Eventually being crowned as [[Archibald]]'s squire leading up to the [[Phandalin Fellows]]' disappearance in [[Wave Echo Cave]], [[Doormat]] channeled his instructor's final wishes into saving [[Phandalin]] and hoping that [[Archibald]] may one day return. Teaming up with [[Droop]], [[Jukha]], and [[Gunther]], they founded the [[Fellas of the Fellows]] in opposition to [[Gundren Rockseeker]]'s rule over [[New Phandalin]].
