A goblin kidnapped by [[Archibald]] after an attempted ambush of them on the [[Triboar Trail]]. Later was given severe blunt force trauma and taken to [[Phandalin]] with the initial trio, trained in the art of [[Dragonchess]], and made [[Archibald]]'s squire. It is during the introduction of this chessboxing ceremony that the goblin was named **Doormat**.
