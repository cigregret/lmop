#### Overview
A brass dragonborn who runs the tavern floor of the [[Stonehill Inn & Tavern]]. A brash, happy fellow who laughs a lot and drinks with patrons at any given chance. A firm lover of [[Dwarves' Blood]], as well as other rare and exclusive alcoholic beverages. Yet, interestingly enough, how he sources his supply is entirely unknown, even to [[Toblen Stonehill]]. Regardless, he makes it work and the [[Stonehill Inn & Tavern]] is all the more profitable for it.

Now publicly known as a member of the [[Zhentarim]], alongside [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]] and [[Jukha]], he directly opposes the [[Lord's Alliance]] and holds a special hatred for [[Gundren Rockseeker]]. Now he is a founding member of the [[Fellas of the Fellows]] alongside [[Jukha]], [[Doormat]], and [[Droop]], channeling his hatred of [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] into this organization created directly to oppose his rule.
