Real Name: **Nezznar**

### Overview
A rougeish drow with strong connections to the Shadowfell, yet supposedly hailing from the Feywilde. Only around 100 years old in physical age, Nezznar's history on the [[Material Plane]] begins and largely centers around [[Wave Echo Cave]] and the [[Sword Mountains]]. Not much else is known, besides that they are a member of the [[Zhentarim]] alongside [[Jukha]] and [[Gunther]]. They also seem to have some ability to overcome death, or at least delay it in herself and her few allies.


### Outdated Information
A mysterious figure who gives orders to [[Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek]], leader of the [[Redbrands]], and [[King Grol]] of the [[Cragmaw Tribe]]. They are interested in the location of [[Wave Echo Cave]], and have supposedly ordered the kidnapping of [[Gundren Rockseeker]] to monopolize this knowledge. 

Through interrogating [[Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek]], the [[Phandalin Fellows]] learned that the **Black Spider** is a female drow who mysteriously rose to power recently. Their suspicions about [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]]'s disappearance are confirmed, as it was under the **Black Spider**'s orders for the [[Cragmaw Tribe]] to capture him and hold him in [[Cragmaw Castle]]. [[Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek|Iarno]] was killed by [[Sildar Hallwinter]] before anything further could be learned.
