A mysterious villain who gives orders to [[Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek]], leader of the [[Redbrands]], and [[King Grol]] of the [[Cragmaw Tribe]]. They are interested in the location of [[Wave Echo Cave]], and have ordered the kidnapping of [[Gundren Rockseeker]] to monopolize this knowledge. 

Through interrogating [[Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek]], [[party name]] learned that the **Black Spider** is a female adventurer who mysteriously rose to power recently. Their suspicions about [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]]'s disappearance are confirmed, as it was under the **Black Spider**'s orders for the [[Cragmaw Tribe]] to capture him and hold him in [[Cragmaw Castle]]. [[Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek|Iarno]] was killed by [[Sildar Hallwinter]] before anything further could be learned.
