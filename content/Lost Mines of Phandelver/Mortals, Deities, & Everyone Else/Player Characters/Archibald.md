### Overview
Archibald Fleetwood is a human paladin currently employed by [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] in a joint operation with the [[Lord's Alliance]]. He hails from [[Neverwinter]], as the [[Fleetwood Family]] is a respected noble house there. However, Archibald was publicly exiled from nobility due to his tendencies to host glamourous parties without attention to budget. After costing his family a large fraction of their wealth, he now works as a hired mercenary.

As a paladin, he has sworn his Oath to [[Bahamut]], the draconic god of justice. His Oath, specifically an Oath of Conquest, strikes fear into the heart of evildoers and even enemies in foreign realms. However, he seems to have no specific goal to the common observer, instead just content with adventuring as part of the [[Phandalin Fellows]].

It is also rumored that he fluently speaks and understands Orcish, leading to some citizens of [[Phandalin]] and other historically warring areas to have a general wariness towards his stature. However, firsthand accounts state that he only discriminates against lower economic classes, and is strongly against interspecies hatred and conflict.
