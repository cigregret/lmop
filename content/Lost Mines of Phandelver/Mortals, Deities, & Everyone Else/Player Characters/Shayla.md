### Overview
**Shayla** is a [[Half-Elf|half-elf]] with rouge-ish tendencies. Hailing from [[Waterdeep]], she is a [[Lord's Alliance#Organization|Stingblade]] of the [[Lord's Alliance]] and is rumored to be the adoptive daughter of [[Sildar Hallwinter]]. Little information is factually known about her, besides that **"Shayla"** is not her real name. 

She currently has been dispatched by the [[Lord's Alliance]] to [[Phandalin]] along with [[MGs]] and [[Sildar Hallwinter]]. The reported purpose of this mission is to assist [[Gundren Rockseeker]] of [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] in reopening the [[Forge of Spells]], presumably to bring economic prosperity to the central [[Sword Coast]]. Has been frequently seen conversing with a goblin named [[Droop]], of which their relationship is unknown.

In her interview to become a [[Lord's Alliance#Organization|Crow]], she notably said nothing at all. However, she was a unanimous decision to undergo training and become part of the [[Lord's Alliance]]. Some raised objections, but those criticisms were quickly silenced seemingly out of nowhere.
