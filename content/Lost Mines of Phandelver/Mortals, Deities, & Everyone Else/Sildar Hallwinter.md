Also known as **Sildar the Chosen**

### Background
**Sildar Hallwinter** is a male human paladin from [[Waterdeep]] who is of the Lioncrown rank in the [[Lord's Alliance]], and the trusted advisor of the late [[Open Lord Garakh Silverhand]]. As a paladin is unknown what [[Oaths|Oath|]] he has sworn, but he is said to embody the spirit of the [[Lord's Alliance]] fully. His reportedly empathetic attitude towards those who have been trodden upon motivates his efforts to lift the poor and troubled through economic reform. He is also a master swordsman, and current owner of the [[Holy Avenger]]. He has taken it upon himself to train promising new [[Lord's Alliance#Organization|Crows]] in the [[Lord's Alliance]] and bring them along for basic missions.

Currently, the [[Lord's Alliance]] has appointed **Sildar** to travel with [[Gundren Rockseeker]] of [[Rockseeker Brothers Inc.]] to [[Phandalin]]. The purpose of this journey is to reopen the [[Forge of Spells]] and bring economic prosperity back to the central [[Sword Coast]]. **Sildar** sent for two [[Lord's Alliance#Organization|Crows]] to meet him there: [[Shayla]] and [[MGs]].

### Rumors
**Sildar Hallwinter** was given the nickname **Sildar the Chosen** for an unknown singular reason. It is said that members of his former adventuring parties were taken aback by his strength and devotion to his [[Oaths|Oath|]], which could even be mistaken for reckless brutality. While common for a paladin to frequently utilize their divine smite, there are no documented examples of **Sildar Hallwinter** using his divine smite. However, those who claim to have seen it state it to not be the standard flash of holy golden light. Instead, his smite crackles with black lightning, sapping the light from any nearby sources and dimming the space around him.

It is also rumored that **Sildar Hallwinter** occasionally performs clandestine missions for the [[Lord's Alliance]], the subject of which are darker than his personality would ever dim. It is during these rumored missions that unreliable accounts are made of his divine smite, personality change, and questionable morality.
