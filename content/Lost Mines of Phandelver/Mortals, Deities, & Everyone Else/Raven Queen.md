### Overview
The goddess of death and the deity in charge of the judgement of souls within the [[City of Judgement]]. She is the de facto leader of the [[Shadowfell]] since its creator, [[Shar]], left long ago. No one knows exactly where the **Raven Queen** came from, nor if it is even her real name. Yet, everyone knows to speak about her in hushed whispers. In legend, she is said to see through the eyes of her pet raven, a being of minor godhood itself that can easily travel throughout planes and witness events unfold through the fabric of space. 

She is known as a mysterious, yet not inherently evil deity. She has been known to make pacts with mortals, binding them as warlocks in her service; yet, there is no publicly known way to contact her or reach the [[Shadowfell]] if one was interested in doing so. 


### Hexblades
Most notably, and best documented, she is or employs an impossibly talented blacksmith who is able to create weapons known as *Hexblades*. The forging processes are unknown, yet it is rumored that the souls of warlocks past who tried to break their contracts are turned into these weapons for warlocks future. Regardless, these weapons are impossibly rare and stronger than often imaginable. They will only react in the hands of one who has a pact with the **Raven Queen**, and are otherwise magically un-wieldable.
