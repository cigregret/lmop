Known as "**Glasstaff**" to [[Redbrands]] and [[Cragmaw Tribe]]
Known as "**Iarno Albrek**" to those within the [[Lord's Alliance]] and to the [[Black Spider]]
**Deceased**


This mysterious human wizard whom was obsessed with invisibility was the leader of the [[Redbrands]], before their demise by a band of adventurers commissioned by [[Sildar Hallwinter]] of the [[Lord's Alliance]]. He, and the rest of the [[Redbrands]], are widely assumed to have had strong connections with the [[Black Spider]]. It is said that he admitted to such transgressions when the party interrogated him.

**Glasstaff** was killed after the party captured him and brought him to [[Sildar Hallwinter]] in the [[Phandalin Townmaster's Hall]]. There, [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] used the [[Holy Avenger]] to swiftly decapitate the wizard. While rumors state that [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] and **Glasstaff** recognized each other and briefly spoke before the **Glassstaff**'s execution, nothing concrete is known.
