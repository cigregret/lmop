#### History
A goblin named for the position of his ears, he was found by the party in the [[Redbrands]]' hideout. He was originally a messenger for [[King Grol]] in [[Cragmaw Castle]], but was captured under [[Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek]]'s orders out of fear that he may divulge the location of [[Cragmaw Castle]] or the [[Nezznar, The Black Spider]]. After being "saved" by the [[Phandalin Fellows]], he pledged his roguish servitude to [[Shayla]] in exchange for food and alcohol whenever they go to the [[Stonehill Inn & Tavern]]. 

After (somehow) befriending [[Shayla]], he joined the [[Phandalin Fellows]] leading up to their disappearance in [[Wave Echo Cave]]. Left behind with [[Doormat]] and [[Jukha]], he swore to carry out her goals in the only way he knew how; forming a then tentative alliance that has now grown to be the [[Fellas of the Fellows]], an organization dedicated to removing [[Gundren Rockseeker]] from power in favor of turning [[New Phandalin]] over to its oppressed populations.
