A goblin named for the position of his ears, he was found by the party in the [[Redbrands]]' hideout. He was originally a messenger for [[King Grol]] in [[Cragmaw Castle]], but was captured under [[Iarno 'Glasstaff' Albrek]]'s orders out of fear that he may divulge the location of [[Cragmaw Castle]] or the [[Black Spider]].
