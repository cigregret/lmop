### Phase 1: Weatherblessed Tombguard
**"Desecrators of the mausoleum; you have defiled my people beyond reconciliation. By entering my domain, you have already forfeited any chance of survival. As the one designated to guard these tombs, I shall now return you to the fires of your creation."**

- Goes second in initiative to the black spider
- Wielding long katana and wearing heavy armor that appears almost cyborg in nature
- Appears to be an automaton
	- does not speak except at the beginning and the name of each move he uses.
- Uses listed attacks in order:
	- Gust Cutter: Weatherblessed Tombguard makes a crescent katana slash at a blistering speed that unleashes a sharp wind past the blade's reach. 
	- Cloud Snatch: Weatherblessed Tombguard easily grapples an enemy with one hand, then throws them with an extended cloud hand.
	- Tempest Crash: Weatherblessed Tombguard summons lightning to strike the points at which enemies are standing, giving little to no indication as to whom is targeted until its too late.
	- Dark Deluge: Weatherblessed Tombguard calls forth an icy tsunami to crash down upon a large area, knocking down those unable to withstand its strength.

### Phase 2: WT + Joka Zimwi
**"O' Dragon of cloud, I split the heavens for your arrival. Lend me your aid, o' dragon of thunder, to fulfill our contract once and for all. Return to the mortal plane, o' dragon of tide, for once more these mortals have forsaken our grace. I call upon thee, o' dragon of storm, to act as my mount once more. Joka Zimwi, return from your disgraceful slumber and lay these defilers to rest!"**

After hitting zero HP, spends an entire turn performing an esoteric rite to summon a wyrmlike dragon known as Joka Zimwi. The party should try to keep them separated, but they'll probably learn that once the first hurricane hits.
- priority is linking up with weathermon, black spider urges to not let it happen and keep weathermon distracted and kill the dragon asap, takes the lead in doing so.
	- otherwise Joka attempts aoe based physical attacks, unless enemy is in melee range of its mouth in which case dragon tries to grapple them and fly up in the sky to drop them
- Tombguard's armor begins to crack on its own, not directly linked to damage done by pc's
	- undead skin begins to be visible, smell of rotting flesh is perceivable by melee range enemies
	- still using same attacks but slower, unless they let him make the hurricane
- Tombguard takes no damage during phase 2, so "killing" Joka Zimwi advances the fight to phase 3. Once Joka is gone, Tombguard busts out the speech of speeches.




### Phase 3: M'Kallu, Soulstorm Guardian
**"Trespassers of holy land; I know not your intentions, but your strength is clear. Allow me to cleanse any hope you may still grasp onto and ensure these holy grounds remain hidden as long as time itself exists. No matter your desires, whether out of genuine kindness or colonizing greed, you know not of the future I've seen. You know not of the horrors to come, of the day when devils walk amongst humans. You know not of the future in which wars are fought for the sake of greater exploitation. You know not that if I do not kill you now, then that future will be set in stone due to the mistakes you have already made."
"Yet, despite my attempts to scare you off, I still see true conviction in each of your eyes. No matter if it be the eyes of a gladiator, an animator, a veteran, a spy, an oathsworn, you all believe in not only your own goals, but those of your allies. Thus, out of respect, I must address you once more. I am the Weatherblessed Tombguard no longer. I fight now as Shepherd of Graves. I fight now as Heavenpiercer. I fight now as Demigod of Orc.  I fight now as the Soulstorm Guardian, M'kallu."**

can only win by either (SOMEHOW BEYOND MY IMAGINATION) countering the super attack, or by putting immovable rod inside its chest to prevent it from moving, at which point it shuts down from "attack mode". 
- zombie flesh and smell is fully perceivable, but still doesnt have health. effective attacks against undead (radiant) stun it for its next turn and chip more armor away
- begins by "killing" the black spider
	- she self revives with a tearjewel (rez item) and gives party members one each
		- tearjewel gives reaction to throw it up to self-rez if you were dealt a killing blow on enemy's turn, or use a bonus action to throw it at an ally (rez at 100% health, single-use consumable)
