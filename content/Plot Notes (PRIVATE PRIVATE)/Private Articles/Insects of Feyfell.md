#### History
Mysterious mosquito-like insects that glow a faint blue-ish purple. Hence the name, they hail from the long-gone overlap between the [[Shadowfell]] and [[Feywilde]] domains. Due to the nature of this area, these bugs are the only creature that could be considered "living" that occupied this zone. Mortals and immortals alike avoided the "[[Feyfell]]" at all costs, as no matter one's stature or power it would spell certain loss. 

When the [[Feyfell]] split in the year 999, the insects scattered and have not been regularly catalouged since. There have been freak accidents that could be attributed to their powers, but the insects use themselves as the catalyst for their effects and thus no reliable traces can be documented. 

#### Effects
The bugs are considered to be beings that draw power simultaneously from both the [[Feywilde]] and [[Shadowfell]], almost a representation of the pure clash between the moral beliefs of those planes' native creatures. When they latch onto a living creature, they instantly melt it into a puddle of slimy goo. This goo is has several documented colors, with the prevalent theory being that it brings out whatever color best matches the creature's aura (via [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools]]). Nothing is known about what power it draws upon that allows it to perform this act, but all creatures that are turned into this goo are soon reborn as apparitions of themselves, with all memories intact. However, these creatures are neither alive nor dead, and spread chaos however their now-perverted personality sees fit. This still raises many questions to those familiar with the bugs on what their powers from the [[Shadowfell]] would be, yet no Archfey nor Lord of Dread could ever work together to provide information.

An instance of this is [[MGs]]' early presence being a cultivated insect experiment performed by [[Cerise, The Prince of Fools]]. It used the one insect he had remaining from the [[Feyfell|Feyfell's]] split a thousand years ago. Through his godly magic, he was able to create an effective simulacrum of [[MGs]] that could have the insect implanted within. This double turned into a blue-colored goo, and was sent to travel with the [[Phandalin Fellows]] as a test run. Meanwhile, the "real" [[MGs]] resided within his home in the [[Feywilde]] and awaited the conclusions.
