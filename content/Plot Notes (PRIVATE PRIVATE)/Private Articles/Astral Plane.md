#### Overview
An infinite plane of raw primordial energy that exists outside of time and space. Leaving this plane dead is considered extremely fortunate, as the most common outcome is for one's body, mind, and soul to be continually ripped apart and put back together for all of eternity. Leaving alive is out of the question for mortals, unless they possess connections with deities strong enough to resist the plane's effects for long enough to provide an escape route.
