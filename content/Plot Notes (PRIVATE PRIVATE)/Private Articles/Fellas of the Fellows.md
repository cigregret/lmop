#### Overview
A rebellious organization native to [[Phandalin]], who stand for only one thing: to continue th efforts and wishes of the beloved folk heroes: the [[Phandalin Fellows]]. Founded by [[Jukha]], [[Doormat]], [[Droop]], and [[Gunther]], their only goal is to overthrow the capitalist overlord of [[New Phandalin]]: [[Gundren Rockseeker|Venerated Lord Gundren Rockseeker]]. They are backed by the [[Zhentarim]] thanks to [[Jukha]] and [[Gunther]]'s efforts, especially due to [[Gundren Rockseeker]]'s standing as one of the [[Lord's Alliance#Organization|Lioncrowns]] of the [[Lord's Alliance]].
