#### Overview
A war of information and spy networks as opposed to outright military power, caused by  [[Gundren Rockseeker|Venerated Lord Gundren Rockseeker]] seeking to usurp the entire [[Lord's Alliance]] from its founder, [[Open Lord Garakh Silverhand]] of [[Waterdeep]]. [[Gundren Rockseeker|Venerated Lord Gundren Rockseeker]] holds the most raw military power and wealth due to his monopoly of magical items on the [[Sword Coast]]. However, his demeanor leads to him having few allies that aren't simply bought out, whereas [[Open Lord Garakh Silverhand]] has numerous allies built on decades of trust and dedication to her goals, and still holds control of the clandestine special forces of the [[Lord's Alliance]].
