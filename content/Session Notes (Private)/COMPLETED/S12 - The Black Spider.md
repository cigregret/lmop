**PROCEED THROUGHOUT THE ADVENTURE AS NORMAL**

### Room 16
- The source of the echoes, there is a small rocky beach with waves lapping the surf. A single tombstone sits on the water's edge, with a rotten corpse kneeled in front of it with its hands in prayer.
- Touching the corpse initiates the boss fight.

### Room 8
- waves of tombstones overrun with fungi, assorted weaponry laying everywhere.
- cave paintings on the walls- in order:
	- the first depicts a small orc facing a green dragon
	- the second depicts an orc warrior holding a bolt of lightning, riding a green dragon over the sea
	- the third is an elf giving the orc the hilt of a sword in the middle of thick woods, with a wooden anvil in the center
	- the fourth is the orc fighting the dragon with a lightning sword and wielding the elements of storm
	- the final shows the orc sitting on the severed head of the dragon, praying to the tombstones with the hilt in his hand
#### Puzzle Time!
- putting a sword hilt into the space on the third painting reveals a hidden button that opens a small space of the wall it's on. within this space is a singular book with an amulet neatly wrapped around it, and one gold rings.
#### Rewards
- book can be read by anyone proficient in Orcish, is titled "Legend of M'Kallu". Reads like a fairytale
- amulet has a sigil of a dragon coiled around lightning, DC10 arcana check shows that it is a magical item
	- Amulet of Storm's Cover (use Ring of Lightning Resistance)
- ring has a sigil of a skeleton, DC10 arcana check shows they are magical
	- Ring of Relentless Endurance (once per dawn, if you drop to zero hitpoints and dont outright die, instead of falling incapacitated you drop to one hit point)


#### The Legend of M'Kallu
- Prof. in Orcish required to read. Reads like a fairytale with no names.
	- "On the day the heavens split, a green dragon of wishes emerged above the beach. It said it could make anything any of them wanted come true, as long as they fulfilled its three requests. One woman said she wanted to try. The dragon asked her to bow. She bowed. The dragon asked her to do a twirl. She twirled. The dragon asked her to pat its nose. She patted. The dragon then told her to ask for what she wanted. Before she could answer, the woman's child spoke up and asked to have the power to protect his mother. The dragon smiled, and replied with only three words: "As you wish." 
	- Many years he trained and prepared to protect his people, as he mastered the ancient gift bestowed by the dragon. Wielding the storm's fury, the child rode the green dragon of wishes to survey the lands for any threats. However, the child still did not find themself strong enough to protect everyone without relying on the dragon. The child heard the whispers of those who wanted their own wish from the green dragon, yet the child forgot it.
	- Decades passed, and the child began to grow old. Remembering an old apothecary that had helped the illness of aging before, the child left their people's safety to the dragon. A half-day's journey later and the child arrived to the apothecary's garden. The apothecary laughed at the child's concerns of age, yet keenly listened to the child's wish of the dragon. The apothecary gave a hilt to the child, telling them that they could not only cure themselves of aging, but protect their family forever with it. However, the child was too ecstatic to listen to the apothecary's warnings before departing.
	- Another half-day's travel later, and the child returned to the village. Noticing it was strangely empty, the child returned to the dragon's residence on the beach to share the good news. As they arrived, they saw the whole village on the beach, screaming demands at the dragon. The child looked on, horrified, before realizing what they must do. Taking up their new hilt, the child charged into battle against the dragon to prevent any more wishes from being made. The child tried to use the very powers granted by the dragon against it, channeling the storm's fury into reopening the heavens and banishing the dragon back to where it came.
	- After seven days and seven nights of warring, on the eve of the longest sun of the year, the child succeeded in splitting the heavens. The green dragon of wishes had its head severed and was silenced. The child cried, losing the only friend it ever truly had. As it tried to cast the hilt into the sea, they realized that they could not let it go. Tears still streaming, the child began to pray. As life around them was birthed, as bodies around them were buried, the child prayed. As mountains grew around the child, the tears they shed created underground waves. As the cavern grew dark, the child locked the bloody beach behind the heaven's split and kneeled at the door. Praying, as the child's body rotted.

### Room 19
- [[Black Spider]] is sitting, calmly talking to [[Jukha]], with four 3-foot spiders around them and hundreds- maybe thousands- more lining the floor and walls. The entire room is a shifting mass of arachnids, their legs producing a chittering sound as they constantly reorient themselves. As the party (or part, for that matter) enters, [[Jukha]] turns and greets them, introducing them to the [[Black Spider]] before they stop him, stating:
	- [[Black Spider]]: **"Thank you, Jukha, but I promise that I am well aware of them and vice versa. Regardless, it is nice to finally meet those who have been trashing my efforts. Please, have a seat so we can all chat together."**
- The spiders move out of the way of any movement that would touch them, then move back once the space is clear again. If the party says anything about the spiders everywhere, the [[Black Spider]] states the following:
	- [[Black Spider]]: **"Oh, right- sorry, I forget that some are arachnophobic. Sadly, this is the only way to keep us from being monitored at the moment, so you'll have to bear with it for now."**
	- Should they ask about the "monitoring":
		- [[Black Spider]]: **"Well, thats exactly what we are about to talk about if you would please enter so I can close the door. I'm sorry to be rude about it, but we don't have much time."**
- Once they enter and have a seat, the [[Black Spider]] thanks the party for arriving and motions for [[Jukha]] to catch them up to speed:
	- [[Jukha]]: **"From what I have learned of your relationship with Nez- I mean, the Black Spider, I assume you are very confused and probably feel somewhat threatened. Allow me to explain."**
		- **"Remember the Historical perspective of Wave Echo Cave that I shared with you all? Well, in my short time here I can fully confirm its legitimacy. The fungal grotto, the tombstones, the documents, and of course the waves. Most of all, the Black Spider lived through the war 100 years ago and witnessed the so-called 'orc invasion'. They theorized that the orcs seen by Phandalinites were simply dopplegangers or polymorphed creatures used to sow fear and shift blame. With the limited magic I know, I was able to identify the bones of many creatures that simply should not have been within the walls of this cave. Small fowl, fishes, even owlbears and turtelk."**
		- **"Now, I know you're thinking that this is still too suspicious to believe. I get it. But please, listen for a bit longer. I have known the Black Spider, as you call them, for years now. I was not aware of your relationship with them, at least not until a couple days ago, but they are not your enemy. We both were worried about how you would react, so we brought in another citation of goodwill. Ne- ahem, Black Spider, if you will."**
- The [[Black Spider]] sighs, before pulling out a mirror. If the party reacts to the mirror (ie assuming that it is the Glass), they simply state the following:
	- [[Black Spider]]: **"No, this is not the Mirror of Hazy Whispers. Yes, you can freely say what you want here. We will talk about the Great Relics later. For now..."**
- They then begin to cast some spell on the glass of the mirror, before it clears and they can see the roof of a well-lit building. They also can hear the sounds of a deafening party, before a familiar brass dragonborn picks up the viewing point and speaks:
	- [[Gunther]]: **"AURGHH WOOOO! HEY NEZNEZ!! AND ARE THOSE- *burp*- MY OWN PhANDALIN FELLAS? BEEN A WHILE SINCE I SAW YOU FOLK AROUND HERE!"**
	- [[Black Spider]]: **"Gunther... *sighs*... are you drunk? I asked you for a favor, remember?"**
	- [[Gunther]]: **"OH RIGHT! I REMEMBER NEZ DON'T WORRY. FELLAS! LISTEN UP! NEZNEZ, JUKHA, AND I ARE PART OF THE ZHENTARIM, AND WE HAVE BEEN TRYING TO ARRANGE THIS MEETING FOR A- *burp*- WHILE NOW! YOU CAN TRUST THEM, JUST LIKE YOU CAN TRUST ME, SINCE I KNOW AT LEAST ONE OF YOU DOESN'T TRUST YOU-KNOW-WHO. ANYWAYS, NEZ AND JUKHA WILL EXPLAIN THE REST! COME GET A BEER- *burp*- OR FIVE ONCE YOU'RE DONE, ALRIGHT?"**
- The mirror goes black, leaving the [[Black Spider]] and [[Jukha]] sitting awkwardly to explain everything. They both glance at each other, sigh, before saying:
	- [[Jukha]]: **"Well... can I stop saying Black Spider now? It was kinda awkward..."**
	- They shoot him a look, before sighing and saying: **"Fine J. Go ahead. For the rest of you, my name is Nezznar. Not Nez, not Nezzie, Nezznar. And, as you heard, I am part of the Zhentarim, also known as the Black Network. We could not contact you sooner due to your... habit of plane-hopping and insistence on clinging to Sildar the Chosen like a baby to its blanket. That fallen angel deserves to be put down, if anything, but nooo, you all just had to follow him like a bunch of baby ducks. And then on top of that, you got Glasstaff killed, as well as fucking up all the prep work I did to have the goblins attack Phandalin so that the orcs could protect them and fix the whole mess so that the burial grounds wouldn't be monopolized for some sick fuck to make a profit off of-"**
	- They trail off and start pouting almost, as [[Jukha]] clears his throat: **"Well, as you can certainly tell, we're a bit of a lively bunch, but our mission is to help the little guy. In this specific mission, that would be my people. When Nezzie and Gunther showed up a couple years ago they wanted nothing more than to help us. I ended up joining to do what I could, but as you know nothing has really happened. At least, until you all arrived to this quaint countryside and started stirring the pot a new one. (Gesturing at the spider-room) All of this keeps the cultists and Sildar from listening in on us while we talk. So, if you have any inquiries, please ask freely. We have all talked quite enough, and are done pouting over our grand schemes."** He states the last part while looking at [[Black Spider|Nezznar]].
- The party can ask questions now. The combined knowledge of [[Black Spider|Nezznar]] and [[Jukha]] is able to answer everything about the [[Redbrands]] and [[Cragmaw Tribe]], as well as most everything about [[Wave Echo Cave]] that isn't the [[Legend of M'Kallu]]. However, Jukha does note his suspicions that the infinite well of magical energy is due to a minor deity who was entombed here. However, he has no idea what deity that could be, and if it was related to the orcs.
	- The plan with the [[Redbrands]] and [[Cragmaw Tribe]] was to pit them against the town of [[Phandalin]], then have the [[Zhentarim]] come in with the orcs and help them defend the town. Hopefully, this would earn the trust of [[Phandalin]] and being allow the [[Zhentarim]] to diplomatically incorporate the orcs into the local communities and legal population. This would extend into finding [[Wave Echo Cave]] to secure the Sword of Blind Fog and prevent the cultists from attacking again.
	- They know that the cultists are watching, listening, and monitoring, especially near [[Phandalin]], since the only Great Relic whose location is somewhat known is the Blade and it is in [[Wave Echo Cave]]. The [[Zhentarim]] intends on finding the [[Three Great Relics]] and either sealing them away or returning them to the [[Feywilde]].
		- However, [[Black Spider|Nezznar]] pulls out a box once the [[Three Great Relics]] are mentioned, and opens it when discussing the locations of them. The Blade is here, the Glass is in [[Waterdeep]], and the Book is in this box. No one present knows what any of the [[Three Great Relics]] do, except the Tome of Temporal Paintings. [[Black Spider|Nezznar]] refuses to elaborate on how they acquired the Book, but explain exactly what it does. 
	- They know that the Blade is hidden amongst the source of the waves, and believe that they have figured out how to get to the cavern in question. 
- They then give the ultimatum: [[Zhentarim]] and the [[Heretic Cultists of Bahamut]] know that the Blade is here and the Glass in [[Waterdeep]]. However, only [[Gunther]], [[Black Spider|Nezznar]], and [[Jukha]] know where the Book is, and it is right here. [[Black Spider|Nezznar]] proposes a deal; regardless of how much the [[Phandalin Fellows]] trust them right now, [[Black Spider|Nezznar]] is willing to give them the Book and their help in exchange for the Blade. Their justification is that the Blade is the most directly dangerous of the [[Three Great Relics]], and needs to be securely contained. The [[Phandalin Fellows]] would get the most helpful of the [[Three Great Relics]], the Book, which spits out a painting of a soon-to-come canon event periodically.
	- Regardless of what they decide, after the deal is done then [[Black Spider|Nezznar]] will make sure to take the Blade and leave the Book for the [[Phandalin Fellows]]. 
- After negotiations and conversation is done, [[Black Spider|Nezznar]] reminds them to not speak the names of [[Jukha]] or themself, since they have no doubt that [[Sildar Hallwinter]] is listening. They then hold out their hand, and the spiders begins scuttling and forming a staff up from the floor and down from the ceiling. The four massive spiders begin shrinking, and form four charms that sit along the head of the staff, which begins to harden over and hide the thousands of small spiders that make it up.
- Motioning for silence, the [[Black Spider]] quickly jots down a note that says
	- *"Find your party, we will wait here"*
- They then take the paper back and jot down another note
				- *"Unless you need a babysitter"*
