### Background
Party has been appointed by the Neverwinter Militia service to deliver additional supplies to phandalin at the request of the Lord's Alliance. Bank transport carriage to transport a supplies and a locked case from one town to another. They’ve never actually interacted with [[Lord Protector Dagult Neverember]], all their communication has been with his herald (half-orc veteran named Derog). The transport would have been done by lower ranking soldiers, but they’ve had a serious epidemic of the flu and are short staffed.

They are given a cart and set out on the road. On the road they encounter a trap set by kobolds (10 of them). If talked to, the kobolds are trying to find someone to heal their "friend". However, their intent is to rob the party. If the party FOLLOWS and heals the wyrmling then they are let go and can continue to Phandalin.

### The trap:
The Kobolds have prepared logs to fall across the camp, which will trap the party and cart  
between them. 5 kobolds will be on each side.  
To Notice that Kobolds are stalking them, DC Perception 15, to notice both sides of the trail 20, to see the wyrmling DC 25

Wyrmling: He has been blinded by acid, losing sight and blindsight, so all attacks made by him are made at   disadvantage, and all attacks against him are at advantage. If he was healed, it is restored.

### Heal Wyrmling Route
 if they heal the wyrmling, they get swept off into arena of wild magic. done deal.
